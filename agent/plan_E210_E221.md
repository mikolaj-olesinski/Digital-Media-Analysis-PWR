### Rules for all three packages
- **Shared files:** agents write only their own `src/<name>.py`, `data/processed/<name>_numbers.json`, figures and `agent/vault/E2xx.md`. One integrator (or each package in turn, at the end) adds register rows to `docs/en|pl/experiments.md` and updates `findings.md`, `backlog.md` and the vault index. This avoids merge conflicts.
- **Re-read loader:** each script carries an identical copy rather than importing another package's file.
  - Read `data/processed/{youtube,x,tiktok}_full_fetched.csv` and keep `status=="ok"` rows with views.
  - y = log10(views.clip(1)); log_likes = log1p(likes); log_comments = log1p(comments).
  - On YouTube, NaN likes or comments mean missing: primary = drop the row; sensitivity = fill 0.
  - Brand comes from the raw export via mention_id; set platform "twitter" for x.
- **Baselines:**
  - Transfer: `results_b.targets()` and `train_combos.data()`, with the export X + YouTube ridge (`fe.ridge`, `fe.C2`): FB 0.385 (×2.43), IG 0.424 (×2.65).
  - In-platform: `rt.FINAL` with `em.ENG`: 0.273 / 0.46 / 0.47.
- **CI:** `va.mae_ci` for single numbers, plus a paired brand bootstrap of the MAE difference (as in `src/fb_hybrid_ci.py`, 300 draws) for every new-vs-base claim.
- **Large-post checks:** report bias in the top quintile both **by true y and by predicted y**. Splitting by true y alone exaggerates regression to the mean (E114).
- **Selection leakage (the main risk):** never pick a variant or tune σ, ratios or other settings on FB/IG views and then call it zero-shot. Choose on leave-one-platform-out within TikTok/YouTube/X, or fix the choice in the card first; show FB/IG for every variant.

### WP-A: the re-read as training data and fixing under-prediction of large posts (one agent, run in order)

**E210 `reread_transfer`**: Does same-moment re-read source data improve transfer to FB/IG?
- **Hypothesis:** cleaner labels read at a similar age reduce under-prediction. IG improves by at least 0.03 (paired CI excludes 0); FB does not get worse.
- **Refuted if:** the IG paired CI includes 0, or FB worsens by more than 0.02.
- **Setup:** ridge on C2.
  - Sources: export X+YT (base); re-read X+YT drop-NaN (primary, fixed in the card); re-read X+YT fill-0; re-read X+YT+TikTok; export and re-read stacked.
  - Also re-run the E201 31-combination grid with re-read TikTok/YouTube/X plus FB/IG, so re-read TikTok/YouTube/X are scored as targets too.
  - Score FB and IG with MAE, factor, CI, paired diff, median bias, within ×2, both top-quintile biases, and brand-total ratio (E157 style).
- **Runtime:** under 1 min, local.
- **Expected:** IG about 0.37 (gain); FB flat; X as a target still the worst.

**E211 `reread_age`**: Is E210's gain just post age?
- **Hypothesis:** the re-read helps because its read age (months) matches the FB/IG read age.
- **Refuted if:** export targets moved to the re-read age reproduce less than half of E210's IG gain.
- **Setup:**
  - Per-platform growth exponent b, estimated only from export/re-read pairs of the same post: Δlog10 views ~ b·Δlog10 age.
  - Move each export target to its re-read age, train the ridge, score FB/IG.
  - Also report views-per-like by age bin on the source.
  - Never estimate b from FB/IG.
- **Runtime:** under 1 min, local.
- **Expected:** most of the gain is age (consistent with E101/E203). Even so, this is the explanation to put in the paper.

**E212 `deshrink`**: Can we undo regression to the mean without losing MAE?
- **Hypothesis:** the likes slope is attenuated by measurement noise in likes.
  - (a) Instrumental-variable regression: export log likes instrument re-read log likes, on posts with both reads; y = re-read views. Or SIMEX with reliability λ = cov(x1, x2) / var(x2).
  - (b) Variance matching: p' = p̄ + (p − p̄)·sd(y)/sd(p), with the factor set in the source's brand CV.
  - Either should cut the top-quintile bias (by predicted y) by at least half while costing at most 0.01 MAE on FB/IG.
- **Refuted if:** the instrumented slope is within 5% of the ordinary slope, or MAE gets worse by more than 0.02.
- **Setup:** ridge; X+YT re-read; outputs: FB, IG, the 5 in-platform leave-one-platform-out scores, brand-total ratio, and Kendall rank of brand totals.
- **Runtime:** under 1 min, local.
- **Expected:** MAE slightly worse; totals and tail better. Report it as a totals/tail option, not a new headline.

**E213 `results_b2`**: confirmatory re-score with the chosen spec (run only if E210 or E212 is positive).
- **Setup:**
  - Spec fixed from E210-E212 and written in the card before running.
  - Re-run the `results_b` outputs: 25-post median shift, comments-only, own-platform ridge, CQR 90% on FB (25% of brands for calibration, 20 splits, E145 protocol).
  - New file; do not overwrite `results_b`.
- **Runtime:** under 1 min.
- **Expected:** IG about ×2.36, FB about ×2.4. Coverage on >1M-follower pages still about 0.84.

### WP-B: augmentation, representations and pseudo-labels (one agent; all Kueue)

**E214 `smoter_tail`**: Do synthetic large posts fix under-prediction of large posts?
- **Hypothesis:** SMOTER/SMOGN oversampling of the top decile of source y reduces top-decile bias by at least 0.1 at a cost of at most 0.01 MAE.
- **Refuted if:** it behaves like E57/E180 (bias barely moves, or MAE gets worse by more than 0.01).
- **Setup:**
  - Rarity: y above the p90 of the training fold.
  - Synthesis: interpolate in (log_likes, log_comments, y) between a rare post and one of its k=5 rare neighbours, λ ~ U(0,1). SMOGN variant adds Gaussian noise σ = 0.05·sd.
  - Oversampling ratios 1×, 2×, 5× of the rare set; optionally undersample the middle 50% to half.
  - Models: (a) ridge X+YT re-read → FB/IG; (b) `rt.FINAL` in-platform on ENG with brand-grouped 5-fold CV, **augmenting the training fold only**.
- **Base:** the same models without augmentation.
- **Runtime:** about 5-10 min, Kueue.
- **Expected:** none or small. For ridge this is close to reweighting.

**E215 `aug_transfer`**: Do domain randomization and mixup let a nonlinear model transfer as well as ridge?
- **Hypothesis:** CatBoost loses to ridge in leave-one-platform-out (E61/E131) because trees cannot extrapolate. Augmenting along the trend closes the gap.
- **Refuted if:** augmented CatBoost is still 0.03 or more worse than ridge on at least 3 of 5 targets.
- **Setup:** features C2; trained leave-one-platform-out over TikTok/YouTube/X (export, comparable to E131's 0.31 / 0.61 / 0.65) and X+YT → FB/IG.
  - (a) Trend-consistent randomization: K = 10 copies, x' = x + δ and y' = y + β̂·δ, with δ ~ N(0, 0.5) per copy and platform, and β̂ the source ridge slope fitted in the fold.
  - (b) Like-propensity randomization: x' = a·x + b, with a ~ U(0.8, 1.2) and b ~ N(0, 0.3) per copy and platform, y unchanged.
  - (c) Cross-platform mixup: λ ~ Beta(0.4, 0.4) over X×YouTube pairs, 1× rows.
  - Models: `rt.FINAL` and `MLPRegressor(64, 64, early_stopping)`.
- **Base:** ridge and plain CatBoost.
- **Runtime:** about 10 min, Kueue.
- **Expected:** (a) brings CatBoost close to ridge but no better. (b) and (c) none or worse. For ridge, noise on inputs only flattens the slope, which is why ridge is not augmented.

**E216 `ssl_repr`**: Does a denoising autoencoder or SCARF contrastive representation add signal?
- **Hypothesis:** a representation learned without views from 12.5k export rows plus re-read rows improves in-platform MAE by at least 0.01 or transfer by at least 0.02.
- **Refuted if:** gains are at or below these thresholds or CIs include 0.
- **Setup:** torch CPU.
  - Rich encoder inputs: ENG + english + text_len + 16 e5 PCA components (from `embeddings_e5_cache.pkl`).
  - Transfer encoder inputs: C2 + e5 only, because those are all FB/IG reads have.
  - DAE: 20% swap noise. SCARF: corruption 0.6, InfoNCE τ = 0.5.
  - Encoder: MLP 64-64 → 8-dimensional code; 50 epochs.
  - Downstream: CatBoost on ENG + code (brand CV) and ridge on C2 + code (X+YT → FB/IG).
- **Leakage:** fitting on all rows without views is transductive; also report a strict variant fitted inside each fold.
- **Runtime:** about 15 min, Kueue.
- **Expected:** in-platform at most 0.005 (E11/E65); transfer worse (E100).

**E217 `pseudo_label`**: Does self-training on unlabelled posts help?
- **Hypothesis:** at least one variant gains 0.01 or more with a CI above zero.
  - (a) Transductive: teacher = E210 ridge; student CatBoost on source + pseudo-labelled FB/IG features (views hidden). Noisy-student variant adds input noise σ = 0.1.
  - (b) In-platform X: rows with missing export views (features present) are pseudo-labelled by the fold's teacher and added to training; scored on labelled X with brand CV. The pseudo-labels are checked against the re-read on the 505 posts that have one.
- **Refuted if:** no variant clears 0.01.
- **Leakage:** never use target views; fit the teacher only on training folds; exclude unlabelled rows from test-fold brands.
- **Dropped variant:** the 2,030 ordinary FB posts, because their reactions are stale (E177). Note the reason in the card.
- **Runtime:** about 5 min, Kueue.
- **Expected:** none. A ridge teacher cannot correct its own bias, and E181 says the missing-view X posts differ.

### WP-C: X, big FB accounts and cross-platform authors (one agent)

**E218 `x_reread`**: Does adding the 505 previously view-less X posts change X?
- **Hypothesis:** on the re-read target, a model trained with those posts lowers whole-stream X MAE (the honest number, E181) by at least 0.02, and X becomes a better transfer source.
- **Refuted if:** the CI includes 0.
- **Setup:**
  - `rt.FINAL` on fresh likes/comments/shares + export followers + log age, brand CV on all 2,013 re-read X posts, with and without the 505 in training.
  - Score separately on the 505 and the 1,508.
  - Also X re-read → FB/IG ridge.
- **Do not mix targets:** never score export targets and re-read targets together.
- **Runtime:** about 2 min, Kueue.
- **Expected:** small gain on the 505; overall small.

**E219 `x_zero_split`**: Is X's 52-62% of zero-like posts what breaks X as a transfer source and target?
- **Hypothesis:** dropping zero-reaction rows from the source, or using a two-part model (zero rows get a per-source zero-row median, positive rows get the ridge), improves X-as-source → FB/IG and transfer to X by at least 0.03.
- **Refuted if:** neither moves by 0.03 or more.
- **Setup:** `train_combos.data()` and the re-read; E201 leave-one-platform-out scoring; split results by zero vs non-zero target rows.
- **Runtime:** under 1 min, local.
- **Expected:** a gain for X-only as a source. Transfer to X is still bad on zero rows (views there come from followers).

**E220 `fb_big_borrow`**: Can borrowed large-account data calibrate the FB band for >1M-follower pages?
- **Hypothesis:** adding residuals from re-read TikTok/YouTube/X pages above 1M followers to the CQR calibration pool lifts FB >1M coverage from about 0.84 to at least 0.88. Those residuals are shifted by the FB level offset, estimated only from the calibration brands.
- **Refuted if:** coverage stays below 0.87 or width grows more than 1.3×.
- **Setup:** E145 protocol (20 splits, 25% of FB brands for calibration, Mondrian by tier). Base = E145/E147. Also the E150 large-page point model retrained on re-read sources.
- **Runtime:** under 1 min, local.
- **Expected:** none or small (E146/E147: the gap is not about width).

**E221 `cross_author_norm`**: With normalised handles and names, do authors appear on more than one platform?
- **Matching:** lowercase; strip non-alphanumerics and emoji; drop the suffixes official / tv / yt / hq / channel / global; also take the handle from author_url; ignore missing names before normalising.
- **Gate:** if 2% or more of labelled posts match, test an E70-style shrunk over-performance from the author's other-platform posts (prior from the training fold only, brand CV).
- **Report:** overlap counts only. Never output names or URLs.
- **Runtime:** under 1 min, local.
- **Expected:** stops at the gate. My check found about 41 labelled TikTok/YouTube/X posts; FB/IG names are mostly missing. This closes the idea with evidence.
