# E111 · Brand24's Facebook reach against measured views

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: how wrong is Brand24's Facebook reach? E93 scored it on the 130 hand-check posts; here on all 359 fetched
  FB videos with a positive reach.
- Hypothesis: the rule is a middle-of-the-road estimate, so it over-shoots small posts and under-shoots large ones.
- Refuted if: the error is uniform across the view range.

## Setup
- 359 FB Reels/videos with measured views and `b24_estimated_reach` > 0.
- Error = log10(reach / views); by follower tier, by quartile of measured views, by kind.
- Model for context: ridge on likes + comments trained on X + YouTube.
- Run: `python src/b24_fb.py`.

## Result
| | median ratio | MAE | share off >2x |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand24 rule | **×4.1** | **0.93** | **82%** |
| X + YouTube model | — | **0.41** | — |

- By follower tier: MAE 0.96-1.01 in every tier; median ×3.7-6.9.
- By measured views: **Q1 small ×34 too high** (median 1.53, MAE 1.49, 97% off), Q2 ×4.8, Q3 ×3.7, **Q4 large ×2 too
  low** (median -0.30, MAE 0.62).
- By kind: Reels 0.97, videos 0.83.

## Decision
Brand24's FB reach is ×4 too high on average and 82% of posts are off by >2x, with a clear regression-to-the-mean
pattern: small posts massively over-estimated, large posts under-estimated. The transfer model halves the error (0.41).
This is the strongest measured case for replacing the rule on FB.
