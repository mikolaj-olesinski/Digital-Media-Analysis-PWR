# E78 · Author level without views: the author's other posts in the sample

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: can the author's level be approximated without any views, from reactions on the author's other posts in the sample (any time, with or without views, other brands, other platforms)?
- Hypothesis: other posts' likes per follower track the author's over-performance and give a gain on authors with other posts.
- Refuted if: no gain on posts whose author has other posts.

## Setup
- Leave-one-out columns per platform and author: number of other posts, mean log likes, mean log comments, mean log likes per follower, share with zero reactions, number of other brands; whether the same author name appears on another platform. Final model + these columns, out of fold on the same brand folds; evaluated on all posts and on posts whose author has at least one other post.

## Result
- All posts: -0.002 / +0.000 / -0.000 (CIs cross zero).
- Posts with other posts by the author: -0.047 (-0.086 to -0.007) TikTok, 142 posts; +0.016 (-0.000 to 0.034) YouTube, 662 posts; -0.002 (-0.036 to 0.030) X, 245 posts.
- Only 0.1-0.6% of posts have an author name on another platform.

## Decision
Refuted. Reactions on the author's other posts do not reveal the author's level; on TikTok they even hurt. Together with E71 this means the author's level is visible only from their past views.
