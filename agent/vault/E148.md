# E148 · FB band excluding the largest pages

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the >1M tier is the only under-covered group (E132/E147). Is the rest of the FB band calibrated?
- Hypothesis: yes, and the global 0.925 hides an over-covering majority and an under-covering tail.
- Refuted if: the rest is also under-covered.

## Setup
- FB transfer CQR (5-fold, 25% calibration brands); coverage of the <1M pages only, and the width of their band.
- Run: `python src/fb_tail_followers.py`.

## Result
- Coverage of pages below 1M followers: **0.940**.
- Band width: **1.99** log10 (x98).
- The >1M tier (70 posts) remains at 0.84.

## Decision
The FB band is conservative (0.94) for 83% of posts and under-covers only the 70 largest pages. Report the band for
<1M with confidence and treat >1M separately (no calibrated band, E147).
