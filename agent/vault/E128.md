# E128 · Which graph edges carry signal?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: every graph method so far failed. Is that because the co-posting edges carry no residual signal at all? For
  each edge type (author, brand, hashtag), does the mean residual of the training posts on the other end predict the
  test post's residual?
- Hypothesis: the author edge carries the signal (E68/E119), brand and hashtag do not.
- Refuted if: no edge carries a residual signal.

## Setup
- 5-fold brand CV; inside each training part, out-of-fold residuals (inner 5-fold).
- For each test post: mean residual of training posts sharing its author / brand / at least one hashtag (no shrinkage,
  at least 2 training posts); Spearman with the test residual; MAE change if the neighbour mean is added.
- Run: `python src/graph_signal.py`.

## Result
| platform | author rho | author MAE change | brand rho | brand change | hashtag rho | hashtag change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.069 | -0.005 | 0.000 | -0.025 | 0.015 | -0.021 |
| YouTube | 0.010 | -0.002 | 0.001 | -0.019 | 0.003 | -0.016 |
| X | -0.015 | -0.039 | 0.009 | -0.053 | -0.029 | -0.066 |

- No edge gives a positive MAE change; the correlations are at most 0.07.

## Decision
Co-posting edges carry no residual signal: propagating residuals along author, brand or hashtag edges always hurts.
This explains why every graph method failed (E03/E06/E12/E29/E65/E122/E127) while the author's *mean views* helps
(E119): the author gain is a level prior inside the model, not diffusion along edges. The graph line is closed for this
data; a real interaction/diffusion graph is needed to reopen it.
