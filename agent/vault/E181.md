# E181 · Correcting X for missing views (inverse-probability weights)

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: 25% of X views are missing, not at random (E105). Does weighting the observed posts by 1 / p(observed | inputs) change the model or the error estimate?
- Hypothesis: The weighted model predicts the unobserved posts better and the honest error is higher than reported.

## Setup
- CatBoost classifier p(view observed) on followers, reactions, flags, check age, year (brand CV); weights 1 / p clipped at 0.05. Naive vs weighted CatBoost MAE; MAE re-weighted to all posts; level predicted on posts without views.
- Part of the critical review batch: `python src/critical_review.py` (via Kueue; E178 locally).

## Result
- Observed 72%, selection AUC 0.88, effective sample share of the weights 0.50.
- Naive model MAE 0.469; **re-weighted to all X posts 0.494**. Weighted model 0.478 / 0.500 (no better).
- Posts without views are predicted at mean log10 1.27 (naive) / 1.28 (weighted) vs 1.53 observed: about x1.8 fewer views.

## Decision
Partly supported. Weighting does not improve the model, but the honest X error for the whole stream is ~0.49, not 0.47; and the posts Brand24 has no views for are predicted ~x1.8 smaller. Report X MAE as 0.47-0.49.
