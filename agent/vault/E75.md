# E75 · Views per like implied by the final model

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: what does the final model say in client terms: how many views per like, and how much do views grow when reactions double, by follower and reaction tier? How does that compare with Brand24's constants (700 per reaction on X)?
- Hypothesis: views per like fall with the number of likes (sub-linear) and are far below 700 on X.
- Refuted if: views per like are flat across like tiers.

## Setup
- Final model fitted on all posts of a platform; per post implied views per like = 10^prediction / likes (likes ≥ 1); elasticity = change in predicted log views when likes and comments double, per log10(2). Medians by follower tier × like tier (≥ 15 posts); real views per like alongside.

## Result
- TikTok: 50-64 views per like at 1-9 likes, 20-34 at 10-99, 14-25 at 100-999, 11-16 at 1000+; real medians mostly match within 15%.
- YouTube: 17-20 for accounts under 100k at any like tier; 20-47 at 100k-1M; 32-113 above 1M.
- X: 25-32 for accounts under 10k; 50-112 at 10k-100k; 279 at 100k-1M with 1-9 likes (real 638). Brand24 assumes 700 (300 above 10 reactions).
- Elasticity: doubling reactions raises predicted views by ×1.3-1.6 on TikTok (0.4-0.7), ×1.5-2.3 on YouTube and X (0.6-1.2).

## Decision
Supported. Views per like fall with likes on TikTok (50 → 11) and rise with followers on YouTube and X; on X they are 25-110 for almost all posts, 6-28× below Brand24's 700. A simple lookup table (followers × likes → views per like) gives clients a readable version of the model.
