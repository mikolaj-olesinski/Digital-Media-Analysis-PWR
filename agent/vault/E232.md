# E232 · Viral and outlier posts without views: followers and reactions as the only input

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: when views are missing (FB, IG), can reactions, followers and post fields flag viral posts (top 10% of views) and posts whose views are far from what the model expects?
- Hypothesis: viral yes (rank of reactions, E48); outliers no (the residual is author level, E70-E72, E231).

## Setup
- A. Viral = top 10% of views within the platform. Same export platforms: train on the other two, test on the held-out one. FB (402) and IG (642): train on X + YouTube only. Scores: ridge estimate (likes + comments), CatBoost classifier (likes + comments), raw likes. AUC and precision in the top 10% of the score (base rate 10%).
- B/C. Over / under-performer = top / bottom 10% of the residual. B used ridge on likes + comments (inflated by nonlinearity and age); C uses the final model residual (`results_preds.csv`, as in E184), CatBoost classifier on field sets, 5-fold CV grouped by E55 components.
- `src/viral_no_views.py` (seconds, local).

## Results
- A, ridge estimate AUC / precision top 10%: TT 0.98 / 0.82, YT 0.88 / 0.72, X 0.86 / 0.57, **FB 0.94 / 0.68, IG 0.98 / 0.84** (base 0.10). Raw likes are as good (FB 0.94 / 0.70, IG 0.98 / 0.81); the CatBoost classifier is not better and fails out of platform on X (0.32 = inverted ranking, cause not investigated) and IG (0.85).
- B (ridge residual): over-performer AUC 0.71 / 0.90 / 0.69 from all fields; most of it is age and ridge nonlinearity.
- C (final-model residual), over-performer AUC from all fields without age: 0.55 / 0.65 / 0.54 (TT / YT / X), post fields only 0.57 / 0.55 / 0.53; under-performer 0.59 / 0.76 / 0.55 (YT: zero-reaction posts).

## Decision
Supported. Viral posts are found from reactions alone, also on FB and IG with a model that never saw them; likes rank as well as any model. Posts that are far above (or below) the estimate cannot be flagged from followers, text or time: AUC 0.53-0.65 against 0.5 chance (YouTube under-performers 0.76 are the zero-reaction posts). The first B run looked strong and was an artefact. Viral = top 10% within the platform; an absolute threshold was not tested. Presentation (backup / applications), no change to the model.

## Follow-ups
- Absolute threshold (e.g. >= 100k views) on FB / IG; a precision-versus-alerts curve.
