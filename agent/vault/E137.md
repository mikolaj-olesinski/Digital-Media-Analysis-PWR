# E137 · A 50% "likely range"

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the 90% CQR band is wide (x14-117). Would a 50% band be a better "most likely" range for a client, and is
  it calibrated?
- Hypothesis: about 50% coverage with a much narrower band.
- Refuted if: the 50% band does not cover 50%.

## Setup
- Same CQR conformity scores as E118; the band is widened by the 50th instead of the 90th percentile.
- Coverage and width on the held-out brands, per platform.
- Run: `python src/cqr_50.py`.

## Result
| platform | 50% coverage | 50% width | 90% coverage | 90% width |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.495 | **0.45** | 0.889 | 1.14 |
| YouTube | 0.483 | **0.74** | 0.902 | 1.71 |
| X | 0.500 | **0.88** | 0.891 | 2.07 |

- 50% widths are x2.8 / x5.5 / x7.6.

## Decision
Report two ranges: a 50% "likely" band (x2.8-7.6) and a 90% band (x14-117). Both are calibrated. The 50% band is the
useful one for a quick sentence; the 90% band is the honest worst case. Add to the reporting guide.
