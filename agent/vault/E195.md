# E195 · Third-party mentions at scale: 40 brands, 144 videos

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: with more brands, does the mention-population result (E191/E193) get tighter, and does the top stratum
  improve?
- Hypothesis: low/mid stay around 0.55-0.6 and the top stratum improves as the sample widens.
- Refuted if: low/mid degrade or the top stays above 1.3.

## Setup
- Same collector (`src/external_youtube_mentions.py`) with 40 brands; four mention-like queries each; brand channels
  excluded; top/mid/low strata; upload 2025+, duration <= 15 min.
- Model trained on Brand24 YouTube; scored with `src/external_validate.py`.

## Result
| quantity | E193 (20 brands) | **E195 (40 brands)** |
|---|---|---|
| videos | 73 | **144** |
| zero-view | 4 | **4** |
| below 100 / below 1,000 | 30 / 39 | **49 / 67** |
| median views | 613 | **2,151** |
| validation rows | 66 | **137** |
| MAE | 0.737 | **0.638** |
| bias | +0.417 | **+0.351** |
| MAE low / mid / top | 0.574 / 0.581 / 1.385 | **0.558 / 0.553 / 1.047** |
| calibration slope | 1.303 | 1.215 |
| median baseline MAE | 1.782 | 1.726 |

## Decision
The result tightens with more brands: on the mention population the model is at **0.558 (low) / 0.553 (mid)** and the top
stratum improves to **1.047** (~x11 low), overall **0.638** with bias +0.35. This is the strongest external evidence so
far that the model transfers to mention-like posts; the remaining problem is the size boundary, not the population.
