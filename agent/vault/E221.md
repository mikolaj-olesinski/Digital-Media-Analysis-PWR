# E221 · With normalised names and handles, do authors appear on more than one platform?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E120 matched raw author names and found 0-0.1% of posts with the author on another platform. With
  normalised names and handles from the profile URL, and with FB / IG authors included, is the overlap large enough
  to use an author's other-platform views as a prior?
- Hypothesis (falsifiable): at least 2% of labelled posts (TikTok / YouTube / X with views, FB 402, IG 642) have an
  author whose normalised key appears on another platform.
- Refuted if: under 2% of labelled posts match. Then the gated E70-style test is not run.

## Setup
- Normalisation: drop missing names before normalising; lowercase; strip everything that is not a-z / 0-9 (emoji,
  punctuation, spaces); drop the suffixes official / tv / yt / hq / channel / global; keys shorter than 3 characters
  ignored. Handle = first meaningful path segment of author_url (leading @ removed, generic segments such as
  channel / user / c / profile.php skipped), normalised the same way.
- Keys: name, handle, and the union. A post matches if any of its keys occurs on a different platform.
- Gate: >= 2% of labelled posts match -> shrunk over-performance from the author's other-platform posts (prior from
  the training fold only, brand CV). Otherwise stop.
- Output: counts only. No names, handles or URLs are written anywhere.
- Run: `.venv/bin/python src/cross_author_norm.py` (seconds, local).

## Result
Labelled posts: TikTok 2,491, YouTube 2,489, X 2,308 (export or re-read views), FB 402, IG 642 (8,332 in all).
Key coverage: names on 100% / 99.6% / 100% / 72% / 8% of posts (TikTok / YouTube / X / FB / IG); handles only on
TikTok, X and IG (8%): YouTube profile URLs carry a channel ID and FB profile URLs a numeric ID, neither a name.

| key | keys on 2+ platforms | labelled posts whose key is on another platform | ... with a labelled post there | share of labelled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| name | 31 (1 on 3+) | 82 (TT 7, YT 28, X 7, FB 30, IG 10) | 56 (TT 7, YT 19, X 3, FB 25, IG 2) | 0.98% / 0.67% |
| handle | 1 | 2 (TT 1, X 1) | 2 | 0.02% |
| name or handle | 31 | 82 | 56 | 0.98% / **0.67%** |

- Gate (>= 2% of labelled posts with a labelled post of the same author on another platform): 0.67%, not passed.
- Only counts are stored; no names, handles or URLs were written.

## Decision
Refuted; the gated E70-style test was not run. Even with normalised names, handles and FB / IG included, 56 of 8,332
labelled posts (0.7%) have an author with labelled posts on another platform, and the few FB matches (25) come from a
handful of keys. This confirms E120 with a wider match: a cross-platform author prior cannot be estimated from this
sample. Close the idea unless Brand24 can link accounts across platforms.
