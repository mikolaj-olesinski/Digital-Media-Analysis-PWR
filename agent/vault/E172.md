# E172 · Brand momentum, past-only (leak-free re-test of E169)

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: do the brand's earlier posts on the same platform (views per reaction over the last 30 days and the whole past) predict a new post's views?
- Hypothesis: a brand on a hot streak keeps over-performing; E169's brand prior may have failed because it was fitted on the training rows' own views.
- Refuted if: paired gains with CIs at or below zero in brand CV and on later months.

## Setup
- Columns: mean log10 views per reaction of the brand's earlier posts on the platform whose view check happened before the post (30 days and all past), and their counts; -1 when none. Coverage 16-24% of posts (the strict check-time rule).
- Every time column uses only other posts whose view check was before the post (no own views, no future).
- Evaluation: final CatBoost (`em.ENG`) vs + columns, same brand folds, paired brand bootstrap; and a temporal split
  (train before 2026-06-01, test after, 1,253-1,588 test posts per platform). Run: `python src/time_momentum.py` via Kueue.

## Result
- Brand CV gain (TikTok / YouTube / X, 95% CI): -0.000 (-0.002 to +0.002) / -0.008 (-0.013 to -0.003) / -0.005 (-0.010 to -0.000).
- Temporal (train before 2026-06-01, test after): -0.023 (-0.034 to -0.015) / -0.066 (-0.081 to -0.046) / -0.026 (-0.040 to -0.013).
- All four groups together: brand CV -0.001 (-0.004 to +0.003) / -0.019 (-0.027 to -0.011) / +0.001 (-0.006 to +0.008); temporal -0.011 (-0.018 to -0.005) / -0.099 (-0.114 to -0.078) / -0.011 (-0.022 to +0.001).
- YouTube's temporal test loses 0.03-0.10 with any added columns (age-at-check shift, E62), so temporal YouTube gains are not informative for small effects.

## Decision
Refuted. A brand's recent over-performance does not carry to its next post, even computed leak-free; the E169 conclusion stands.
