# E199 · Predicting a view range instead of a number

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: If the output is a view range (bin) instead of a number, what accuracy and F1 do we get, and does a direct classifier beat the regression model cut into bins?
- Hypothesis: A direct classifier is no better than the regression cut into bins; bins only change the presentation.

## Setup
- Bin schemes (log10 edges): decades (<10 ... 1M+), 4 user ranges (<100, 100-1k, 1k-10k, 10k+), 3 ranges (<1k, 1k-10k, 10k+), half decades (x3.2 wide).
- In-platform: TikTok / YouTube / X, final features, brand-grouped 5-fold CV. Regression = CatBoost MAE cut into bins; classifier = CatBoost MultiClass, same features; baseline = majority class of the training fold.
- Transfer: trained on X + YouTube (likes + comments), tested on FB (402 fetched) and IG (133 hand-batch reels with visible likes, preliminary).
- Metrics: accuracy, macro / weighted F1, share within one bin, classifier top-probability ECE. `python src/view_bins.py` (via Kueue).

## Result
| accuracy (macro F1), regression cut into bins | TikTok | YouTube | X | FB | IG |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 ranges | 0.82 (0.79) | 0.81 (0.75) | 0.95 (0.45) | 0.80 (0.78) | 0.78 (0.72) |
| 4 user ranges | 0.76 (0.65) | 0.68 (0.69) | 0.80 (0.44) | 0.70 (0.71) | 0.67 (0.68) |
| decades | 0.74 (0.55) | 0.60 (0.55) | 0.58 (0.33) | 0.64 (0.57) | 0.56 (0.46) |
| half decades | 0.53 (0.33) | 0.36 (0.30) | 0.33 (0.19) | 0.39 (0.38) | 0.33 (0.28) |

- Majority baseline accuracy, 3 ranges: 0.59 / 0.63 / 0.95 / 0.45 / 0.58. On X 95% of posts are <1k, so 0.95 is the baseline and macro F1 0.45 is the honest number.
- Within one bin: 0.97-1.00 for decades and coarser; 0.79-0.93 for half decades.
- The direct classifier is lower everywhere, by 0.01-0.08 in accuracy and up to 0.14 in macro F1 (FB decades 0.43 vs 0.57). Its top probability is mildly over-confident (ECE 0.03-0.18, worst with half decades and on IG).

## Decision
Supported. Bins are a presentation of the same model, not a better model: the regression cut into bins beats a direct classifier on every platform and scheme. Accuracy depends mostly on the bin width (3 ranges ~0.8, decades ~0.6, half decades ~0.35) and on the class balance (X). For users, show the regression's range with a band, not a separate classifier.
