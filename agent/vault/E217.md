# E217 · Does self-training on unlabelled posts help?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: can pseudo-labels on posts without views (target-platform features, or X posts whose export views are missing) improve the estimate?
- Hypothesis (falsifiable): at least one variant gains 0.01 or more MAE over its base with a paired CI above zero.
- Refuted if: no variant clears 0.01 with a CI above zero.
- Primary variants (fixed before running): (a) hard pseudo-labels, CatBoost student; (b) hard pseudo-labels on X. Noisy variants are secondary.

## Setup
- (a) Transductive transfer. Teacher = ridge on log likes + log comments, trained on re-read X + YouTube (drop missing reactions; loader copy in `src/wpb_common.py`). Student = `rt.FINAL` on the same two features, trained on the source rows + the target platform's posts (FB 402 or IG 642) labelled by the teacher; target views hidden. Noisy student: N(0, 0.1) on all student inputs. Reference: the student without pseudo-labels (plain CatBoost on the source). Base for the claim: the teacher. Sensitivity: export X + YouTube teacher. A ridge student is not run: it returns the teacher's coefficients.
- (b) In-platform X. Labelled X = export posts with views > 0 (1,803), brand-grouped 5-fold CV, `rt.FINAL` on ENG. Unlabelled = export X posts with missing views (features present; zero-view rows are not used), restricted to brands not in the test fold. The fold teacher (trained on the training fold) labels them; the student trains on the training fold + pseudo rows. Noisy: N(0, 0.1) on the pseudo rows' continuous features. Pseudo-labels checked against the re-read views of the posts that have one, next to the same teacher's error on labelled posts against their re-read.
- Dropped variant: the 2,030 ordinary FB posts (no video id). Their only reactions are Brand24's stale counts (likes about x2 low, comments almost all zero, E177), so pseudo-labels on them would be built on a different feature distribution.
- Paired brand bootstrap (300 draws). Target views never used for selection; all variants reported.
- Queue job: `amc-pseudo`, 4 CPU / 4 GiB.

## Result
MAE of log10 views (factor x10^MAE), brand bootstrap CI; gain = MAE(base) - MAE(variant), paired brand bootstrap, + = better. Job `amc-pseudo-1108-215753`, 4 CPU, about 1 min.

(a) Transductive transfer, re-read X + YouTube teacher (primary):

| variant | FB 402 MAE | gain vs teacher [CI] | IG 642 MAE | gain vs teacher [CI] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| teacher ridge (base) | 0.387 (x2.44) [0.325, 0.457] | - | 0.382 (x2.41) [0.348, 0.420] | - |
| CatBoost without pseudo-labels | 0.381 | +0.006 [-0.008, +0.028] | 0.391 | -0.009 [-0.015, -0.004] |
| student, hard pseudo-labels (primary) | 0.384 (x2.42) | +0.004 [-0.005, +0.013] | 0.384 (x2.42) | -0.002 [-0.005, -0.000] |
| noisy student, sigma 0.1 | 0.380 | +0.008 [-0.003, +0.021] | 0.387 | -0.005 [-0.009, -0.001] |
| student on pseudo-labelled target rows only | 0.391 | -0.003 [-0.008, +0.001] | 0.384 | -0.002 [-0.005, +0.001] |

- Export X + YouTube teacher (sensitivity; teacher FB 0.385 / IG 0.435): every student is equal or worse (FB -0.001 to -0.007, IG -0.003 to -0.024; IG CIs below zero for the source + pseudo students).

(b) In-platform X, 1,803 labelled posts, 634 posts with missing export views (509-552 used per fold after excluding test-fold brands):

| variant | MAE | gain vs base [CI] |
|---|---|---|
| base, labelled only | 0.467 (x2.93) [0.446, 0.489] | - |
| hard pseudo-labels (primary) | 0.470 (x2.95) | -0.003 [-0.005, -0.001] |
| noisy pseudo rows, sigma 0.1 | 0.471 | -0.004 [-0.007, -0.001] |
| pseudo rows at weight 0.5 | 0.469 | -0.003 [-0.005, -0.001] |

- Pseudo-label check: on the 473 view-less posts that have a re-read, the teacher's MAE against the re-read views is 0.658 with median bias +0.37. On the 1,508 labelled posts with a re-read, the out-of-fold teacher has MAE 0.563 and median bias +0.39. The level offset is the same (the re-read is about x2 later), but the error is 0.095 larger on the view-less posts. They are harder posts, as E181 found, and the teacher's labels on them are poor.
- The 2,030 ordinary FB posts were not used (stale reactions, E177).

## Decision
Refuted: no variant gains 0.01 with a CI above zero. The best is the noisy student on FB (+0.008, CI including 0). A ridge teacher cannot correct its own bias, so the student only re-learns the teacher. On X the pseudo-rows add the teacher's errors on a harder subpopulation and cost 0.003-0.004. Pseudo-labelling is closed.
