# E139 · Target definition: views vs views per day

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: views grow with the age of the check. Does modelling views per day (rate) and multiplying back by age beat
  modelling views directly? Does adding log(age) as a feature help?
- Hypothesis: the rate target handles the age variation better.
- Refuted if: no gain.

## Setup
- Age = metrics_updated_at - created_at, clipped to 0.1-3650 days.
- Three variants: direct views; direct + log(age); rate = views/day with log(age) as a feature, multiplied back by age.
- Final CatBoost features, 5-fold brand CV.
- Run: `python src/rate_target.py`.

## Result
| platform | direct | direct + age | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.273 | 0.276 |
| YouTube | 0.456 | 0.454 | 0.458 |
| X | 0.467 | **0.454** | 0.459 |

## Decision
The rate target is not better. Adding log(age) as a feature is a small real gain on X (-0.013, consistent with E44) and
neutral elsewhere; add it for X. The target stays log10(views).
