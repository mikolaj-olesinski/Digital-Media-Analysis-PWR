# E149 · Followers as a feature for large FB pages

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E94 found followers hurt the FB transfer overall. Do they help the >1M pages, where the level is most
  different?
- Hypothesis: yes, followers carry the level for large pages.
- Refuted if: MAE rises.

## Setup
- Ridge on the source platforms with likes + comments, vs likes + comments + log(followers); applied to 402 FB posts;
  MAE on the >1M subset and the rest.
- Run: `python src/fb_tail_followers.py`.

## Result
| features | MAE >1M | MAE <1M | bias >1M |
|---|---|---|---|
| likes + comments | 0.410 | **0.380** | +0.33 |
| + followers | **0.343** | 0.727 | -0.06 |

## Decision
Followers are a large-page feature: on >1M pages they lower MAE from 0.410 to 0.343 and remove the level bias (+0.33 ->
-0.06), but on the rest they blow up the error (0.380 -> 0.727). So E94's "followers hurt" is true only on the whole
sample; use followers only for >1M, not globally.
