# E73 · Brand totals: the error a client sees

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Brand24 clients see the summed reach of a brand. How accurate is the sum of our per-post estimates for a brand (and a brand in one month), and how does Brand24's reach column compare on X?
- Hypothesis: totals are more accurate than single posts (errors partly cancel); summing median predictions underestimates totals; Brand24 reach overestimates X totals by about ×10.
- Refuted if: the total MAE is not below the per-post MAE.

## Setup
- Brands (and brand-months) with ≥ 5 posts with views per platform. Predicted total = sum of 10^prediction (out-of-fold final model); calibrated = times 10^(median brand log ratio true / predicted from the other folds). Error = |log10 predicted total / true total|; bootstrap over brands. On X also the sum of Brand24's reach column. (A mean-based smearing factor was tried first and overestimated YouTube totals by ×15 because of the heavy tail; dropped.)

## Result
| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| brands | 238 | 213 | 189 |
| per-post MAE | 0.273 | 0.456 | 0.466 |
| total MAE, sum of medians | 0.232 (bias -0.10) | 0.338 (bias -0.24) | 0.364 (bias -0.25) |
| total MAE, calibrated | **0.227 (0.20-0.25)** | **0.311 (0.27-0.36)** | **0.317 (0.28-0.36)** |
| totals within ×2 (calibrated) | 73% | 65% | 56% |
| Spearman of totals | 0.93 | 0.86 | 0.82 |
| Brand24 reach, total MAE | | | 1.017 (bias +1.00, ×10 too high) |

- Brand-month totals are about as accurate (0.23 / 0.31 / 0.35).

## Decision
Supported. A brand's total is estimated within about ×1.7 / ×2.0 / ×2.1 (MAE 0.23 / 0.31 / 0.32), better than single posts, and ranks brands well (Spearman 0.82-0.93). Raw sums of medians underestimate totals by ×1.3-1.8 (the total is driven by a few large posts); a one-number calibration from other brands removes this. On X Brand24's reach overestimates brand totals by ×10; our estimate is about 3× more accurate in log terms.
