# E193 · Expanded third-party mention sample (20 brands)

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: does the E191 result (the model works on third-party mentions, fails on the popular stratum) hold on a larger
  sample?
- Hypothesis: the same pattern, with tighter intervals.
- Refuted if: the low/mid strata get much worse or the top stratum improves.

## Setup
- Same collector (`src/external_youtube_mentions.py`), now 20 brands instead of 12; four mention-like queries each; brand
  channels excluded; top/mid/low strata.
- Model trained on Brand24 YouTube; scored with `src/external_validate.py`.

## Result
| quantity | E191 (12 brands) | **E193 (20 brands)** |
|---|---|---|
| videos | 47 | **73** |
| zero-view | 1 | **4** |
| below 100 / below 1,000 views | 17 / 26 | **30 / 39** |
| median views | 362 | **613** |
| model rows (views > 0, followers > 0) | 45 | **66** |
| MAE | 0.791 | **0.737** |
| bias | +0.39 | **+0.417** |
| MAE low / mid / top | 0.690 / 0.441 / 1.569 | **0.574 / 0.581 / 1.385** |
| calibration slope | 1.328 | 1.303 |

## Decision
The pattern holds and tightens: the model is close to the export on the low and mid strata (0.574 / 0.581 against 0.456
CV), and still fails on the popular stratum (1.385, ~x24 low). Zero-view posts are real and reachable (4 of 73). This is
now the best external evidence that the model transfers to mention-like posts, with the size boundary as the remaining
issue.
