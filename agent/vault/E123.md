# E123 · CQR intervals for the Facebook transfer

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E98 gave split-conformal intervals for the FB transfer; CQR is narrower in-platform (E118). Does CQR give a
  calibrated 90% band on FB when calibrated on a quarter of the FB brands?
- Hypothesis: coverage about 90%, narrower than split conformal.
- Refuted if: coverage far off or widths wider.

## Setup
- Quantile models (LightGBM 0.05/0.95) trained on X + YouTube; the CQR band is calibrated on 25% of the training FB
  brands and evaluated on the held-out brands (5-fold brand CV, 402 posts).
- Baseline: split conformal around a median model trained on the sources.
- Run: `python src/fb_cqr.py`.

## Result
| method | 90% coverage | median width (log10) |
|---|---|---|
| CQR | **0.925** | **1.99** |
| split conformal | 0.932 | 2.07 |

- 402 FB posts; 25% of the training brands used for calibration.

## Decision
CQR is slightly narrower and covers a bit above nominal (0.925 at 90%); a 90% band of about ×98 on FB is honest but
wide. For a client who will not collect labels, the source-width band (E98, 1.285 half-width) is the label-free option;
with a few FB labels, CQR tightens it.
