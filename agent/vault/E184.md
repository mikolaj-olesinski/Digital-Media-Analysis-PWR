# E184 · Anomalies: who over- and under-performs the estimate?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Do posts far above / below the estimate mark paid promotion, spam or bought engagement?
- Hypothesis: Over-performers are more often promo; under-performers more often spam.

## Setup
- Top / bottom 5% of the out-of-fold residual vs our AI type labels and duplicate texts.
- Part of the critical review batch: `python src/critical_review.py` (via Kueue; E178 locally).

## Result
| platform | promo: all / over / under | spam: all / over / under |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 34% / **51%** / 31% | 13% / 11% / **26%** |
| YouTube | 43% / 47% / 47% | 15% / 20% / 19% |
| X | 19% / 12% / 12% | 12% / 18% / 11% |

- TikTok under-performers have median 5 reactions (all posts 32).

## Decision
Supported on TikTok only: over-performers are x1.5 more often promo, under-performers x1.9 more often spam. On YouTube / X the residual does not mark either. A TikTok anomaly flag is plausible; there is no ground truth for bought engagement.
