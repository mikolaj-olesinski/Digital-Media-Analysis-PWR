# E43 · How well can views be estimated at posting time, before any reaction?

## Question and hypothesis
Posting-time columns beat constant × followers and followers alone.
Refuted if: no CI above zero over followers-only CatBoost.

## Setup
All TikTok / YouTube / X posts with views (2,491 / 2,441 / 1,803). CatBoost (MAE loss) on followers, text stats, VADER, TF-IDF SVD (30), AI labels, calendar, log horizon (hours to the check), brand size; variant + smoothed author mean from the training fold. Reference with reactions.

## Result
| MAE | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| constant × followers | 0.912 | 0.860 | 0.880 |
| CatBoost: followers | 0.591 | 0.775 | 0.584 |
| CatBoost: posting-time | 0.575 | 0.684 | 0.550 |
| + author history | 0.573 | 0.686 | 0.546 |
| reference with reactions | 0.272 | 0.447 | 0.449 |

Gain posting-time over followers-only: +0.017 (0.007-0.025) / +0.092 (0.072-0.113) / +0.034 (0.019-0.050). Author history: CI includes zero. 90% interval ×20 / ×27 / ×14.

## Decision
A posting-time estimate is possible but coarse; reactions remain the main signal. Author history dropped.

## Follow-ups
