# E222 · Headline in-platform result on author-safe folds

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: one author can write about several brands (1-9% of posts), so brand-grouped folds can put the same author or a copied text in training and test. Re-run the preprint headline (results A) with folds that forbid this.
- Hypothesis: as in E55, MAE moves by at most 0.002 on TikTok and YouTube and about 0.01 on X.

## Setup
- `src/results_a.py`: CatBoost `rt.FINAL` on `em.ENG`, 5-fold GroupKFold on E55 components (brand ∪ author ∪ identical text > 20 chars); the CQR calibration quarter is also split by component. CI: brand bootstrap.
- Base: the same script with brand folds (previous `results_a_numbers.json`).

## Result
| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| brand folds | 0.273 (x1.88) | 0.460 (x2.88) | 0.470 (x2.95) |
| brand ∪ author ∪ text | 0.273 (x1.87) | 0.462 (x2.90) | 0.482 (x3.03) |
| 90% coverage, strict | 0.902 | 0.907 | 0.873 |

## Decision
Supported: TikTok and YouTube unchanged, X 0.012 worse (×2.95 → ×3.03, rounds to ×3.0). The preprint headline now uses the author-safe folds; method comparisons stay on brand folds, where the shift is the same for all methods (E55).
