# E175 · Text cues not covered before: giveaway, call to action, disclosure, mentions, titles

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: do explicit text cues (giveaway / contest, call to action, sponsorship disclosure, @mentions, URLs, questions, other brands named, YouTube title length, caps and clickbait words) explain views?
- Hypothesis: giveaways and calls to action buy engagement without views (more reactions per view), disclosures mark paid reach.
- Refuted if: paired gains with CIs at or below zero in brand CV and on later months.

## Setup
- Regex flags on title + content: giveaway 1%, call to action 11%, disclosure 4%, news / repost 3%; counts of @mentions, URLs, question marks, brand names from the file; title length, caps share, clickbait words.
- Every time column uses only other posts whose view check was before the post (no own views, no future).
- Evaluation: final CatBoost (`em.ENG`) vs + columns, same brand folds, paired brand bootstrap; and a temporal split
  (train before 2026-06-01, test after, 1,253-1,588 test posts per platform). Run: `python src/time_momentum.py` via Kueue.

## Result
- Brand CV gain (TikTok / YouTube / X, 95% CI): +0.002 (-0.000 to +0.004) / +0.000 (-0.006 to +0.006) / +0.009 (+0.002 to +0.016).
- Temporal (train before 2026-06-01, test after): -0.002 (-0.006 to +0.001) / -0.065 (-0.083 to -0.047) / +0.001 (-0.009 to +0.012).
- All four groups together: brand CV -0.001 (-0.004 to +0.003) / -0.019 (-0.027 to -0.011) / +0.001 (-0.006 to +0.008); temporal -0.011 (-0.018 to -0.005) / -0.099 (-0.114 to -0.078) / -0.011 (-0.022 to +0.001).
- YouTube's temporal test loses 0.03-0.10 with any added columns (age-at-check shift, E62), so temporal YouTube gains are not informative for small effects.

## Decision
Small: a significant +0.009 on X in brand CV but not confirmed on later months (temporal CI includes 0); not adopted. Text stays closed except as an ensemble member (E65).
