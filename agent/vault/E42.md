# E42 · Does the forecast interval hold?

## Question and hypothesis
A residual-quantile interval from other brands gives about 90% coverage.
Refuted if: coverage below about 0.85.

## Setup
For each brand, the 90% quantile of absolute out-of-fold residuals of all other brands' posts (E40 CatBoost).

## Result
Coverage 0.90 / 0.90 / 0.90; half width ×2.4 / ×3.8 / ×4.1.

## Decision
Interval is usable at this sample size; width is not conditional on horizon yet.

## Follow-ups
B05 (interval by horizon).
