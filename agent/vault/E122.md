# E122 · Spectral graph embeddings

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: earlier hand-built graph features gained at most 0.009 (E12/E29). Do low-rank spectral embeddings of the
  author-brand bipartite graph do better as features?
- Hypothesis: embeddings capture the community structure more compactly and help a little.
- Refuted if: no gain or worse.

## Setup
- Graph: authors and brands as nodes, an edge for every post (weighted by count); normalised adjacency; 8-dimensional
  SVD embedding for both node types, added to the final CatBoost features.
- 5-fold brand CV; MAE per platform.
- Run: `python src/graph_embed.py`.

## Result
| platform | base | + graph embeddings |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.278 |
| YouTube | 0.456 | 0.479 |
| X | 0.467 | 0.485 |

## Decision
Graph embeddings hurt by 0.005-0.023. Combined with E03/E06/E07/E12/E29/E65, the graph line is closed: the author-brand
structure does not add to followers and reactions. Do not reopen without a real interaction graph.
