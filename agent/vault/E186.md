# E186 · Monthly ranking of brands within an industry

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Does the model rank brands within an industry and month (share of voice) as well as the truth?
- Hypothesis: Kendall >= 0.6 in most industry-months.

## Setup
- From E180: brand-month totals (>= 3 posts) within industry x month groups with >= 3 brands; Kendall of predicted vs true totals; model vs Brand24's rule.
- Part of the critical review batch: `python src/critical_review.py` (via Kueue; E178 locally).

## Result
| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| industry-months | 52 | 51 | 42 |
| model mean Kendall | 0.73 | 0.70 | 0.60 |
| share with Kendall >= 0.6 | 79% | 75% | 60% |
| rule mean Kendall | 1.00 | 1.00 | **0.63** |

## Decision
Supported with a caveat. The model ranks brands within an industry-month well (0.70-0.73 on TikTok / YouTube), but on X the rule ranks slightly better (0.63 vs 0.60) even though its level is x11 off. For share of voice on X the model's value is the level, not the order.
