# E98 · Intervals for the Facebook estimate

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: can we put an honest interval on the FB estimate? Two sources of width: residuals from the source
  platforms, and a split-conformal width calibrated on k FB posts.
- Hypothesis: source residuals over-cover on FB (the FB error is smaller than on X/YouTube); a split conformal on
  25-50 FB posts is close to nominal.
- Refuted if: coverage is far from the nominal level.

## Setup
- 402 FB posts with views, predictions from the E93 ridge (X + YouTube).
- Source width: 80/90% quantile of |residual| from 5-fold brand-grouped CV on X + YouTube.
- Split conformal: k FB posts give the quantile of |residual|; coverage on the remaining posts, 400 draws per k.
- Run: `python src/fb_extend.py`.

## Result
| width from | nominal 80% | nominal 90% |
|---|---|---|
| source-platform residuals | half-width 0.92, coverage **0.925** | half-width 1.285, coverage **0.960** |
| FB conformal, k = 10 | coverage 0.757 | coverage 0.841 |
| FB conformal, k = 25 | coverage 0.774 | coverage 0.867 |
| FB conformal, k = 50 | coverage 0.790 | coverage 0.880 |

- Source widths are conservative on FB: they cover 92.5% / 96% instead of 80% / 90%, i.e. a 90% interval of
  x19-20 is wider than needed.
- FB-calibrated widths are close to nominal but under-cover slightly (0.88 at 90% with 50 posts).

## Decision
For FB, source-based intervals are safe but wide (x8 at 80%, x19 at 90%); with 50 checked posts the width can be
calibrated down to a near-nominal 90% interval. Report intervals, not points (E73, E79). Update findings.
