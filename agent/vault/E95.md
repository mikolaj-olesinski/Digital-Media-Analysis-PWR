# E95 · Facebook beyond the 130-post sample

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: does the E93 transfer hold on all 402 public Facebook Reels/videos (not only the 130 hand-check sample),
  and does the error depend on the kind of video, the age of the post, or on which posts disappeared?
- Hypothesis: the 130 sample is representative; error is flat across kind and age.
- Refuted if: the larger set is clearly worse or the error depends strongly on kind/age.

## Setup
- Target: all 402 FB Reels/videos with a fetched view count (`data/processed/fb_all_fetched.csv`), views = `video_view_count`.
- Model: ridge on log1p(reactions) + log1p(comments), trained on X + YouTube only; same as E93.
- Metrics: MAE log10, brand bootstrap CI, bias, Spearman; by kind; by quartile of post age at the fetch.
- Unavailable: the 68 posts whose page shows no video; compared on Brand24 reactions and followers.

## Result
| subset | posts | MAE | 95% CI | bias | factor | Spearman |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 130 hand-check sample | 130 | 0.345 | 0.281-0.435 | +0.06 | ×2.2 | 0.93 |
| all public FB videos | 402 | **0.385** | 0.312-0.482 | +0.14 | ×2.4 | 0.91 |
| Reels | 280 | 0.383 | 0.303-0.499 | +0.14 | ×2.4 | 0.89 |
| videos | 122 | 0.390 | 0.317-0.468 | +0.14 | ×2.5 | 0.94 |

- By age quartile: 0.352 (young), 0.450, 0.339, 0.401 (old); bias rises with age (+0.05 to +0.16). No clean trend.
- The 68 unavailable posts: median Brand24 reactions 5.5 vs 6.0 measured; median followers 66k vs 110k. Smaller pages
  are more likely to be gone/restricted, reactions are not different. 22 of them are in the 312 sample.
- 402 posts, 20 brands.

## Decision
The transfer is not an artefact of the stratified sample: on the full public FB video set the MAE is 0.385 (×2.4),
slightly worse than on the 130 but with the same conclusion. The model under-estimates FB by ~1.4x on the larger set
(bias +0.14), so a level correction on a few FB posts would trim it.
Update findings.
