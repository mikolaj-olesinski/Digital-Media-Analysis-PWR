# E233 · Independent checks: X views (export vs free vs paid) and YouTube zero likes

## Question and hypothesis
- Question 1: the export, our FxTwitter read and the paid X API disagree on views - which one is wrong?
- Question 2: are the 365 YouTube posts with `likes = 0` in the export true zeros, or missing data?
- Hypothesis: the export is stale (older counts) and the free read matches the paid API; export zeros are often missing.
- Refuted if: the paid API agrees with the export rather than with our read, or the zero likes are real.

## Setup
- X: 50 export posts with `view_count > 0` and a successful re-read; one paid lookup by id
  (`public_metrics`, one call, **$0.25**), compared with FxTwitter and with the export.
- YouTube: 20 export posts with `likes == 0` and a successful re-read; the public watch page was parsed for
  `likeCount` (a different route than yt-dlp).
- Public pages and the paid API only; no login, no model fitted.

## Result
| comparison | identical share | median ratio | Spearman |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| FxTwitter vs paid API | **98%** | **1.000** | **1.000** |
| export vs paid API | 0% | **1.792** | 0.926 |

- YouTube zero-likes sample (n = 20): page shows a like count for 16; of those **10 have positive likes** (e.g. 22) although
  the export says 0, **6 are true zero**, and 4 pages expose no count (`unknown`). Our re-read has positive likes for those
  same 10 and missing (not zero) for the rest.

## Decision
- Our same-moment read is correct; the export understates X views by ~x1.8 (rank still 0.93).
- YouTube zeros in the export are **not reliable**: at least half of the sampled zero-like posts have likes > 0, so a silent
  0 must be treated as **missing**, not as a true zero (supports the data-contract rule and B16).
- No model re-fit here; the numbers feed the validation note and the Brand24 questions.
