# E158 · FB brand totals after calibration

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E157 found FB brand totals ×2 low with 53% of brands off by >2x. Does a one-number calibration (E109 style)
  fix the level, and is an 80% brand interval honest with only 19 brands?
- Hypothesis: the level is fixed, the interval is shaky.
- Refuted if: the interval covers 80%.

## Setup
- 19 brands with >= 5 FB posts; total = sum of 10^predicted; leave-one-brand-out calibration factor and 80% half-width
  from the other 18 brands.
- Run: `python src/fb_brand_cal.py`.

## Result
| | median ratio | share off >2x | Kendall | 80% coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| uncorrected | +0.31 (est low) | 53% | 0.801 | — |
| **calibrated** | **-0.01** | **47%** | 0.789 | **0.737** |

## Decision
Calibration removes the level bias (0.31 -> -0.01) and cuts the off-2x share only from 53% to 47%; the 80% interval
covers 0.737 (below 0.80) with 19 brands. FB brand totals remain uncertain: report the calibrated total with a warning,
or avoid brand totals from so few posts.
