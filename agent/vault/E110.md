# E110 · Posting cadence and reach

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: do brands that post more often get less reach per post (audience saturation)? Across brands and within a
  brand (a post soon after another vs after a long gap).
- Hypothesis: more posts per month mean fewer views per post.
- Refuted if: no relation.

## Setup
- Posts with views (TikTok, YouTube, X); brand-platform groups with >= 5 posts (640 groups).
- Across brands: posts per 30 days vs median log views; Spearman, and after residualising on followers and platform.
- Within brand: gap since the previous post; residual log views after reactions, followers and platform; compare posts
  with a gap < 1 day and > 7 days.
- Run: `python src/cadence.py`.

## Result
- Across brands: Spearman **0.029** (p = 0.46); after followers and platform **0.057** (p = 0.15).
- Within brand (5 944 posts): median residual -0.043 for a < 1 day gap and -0.039 after > 7 days; difference **-0.004**.
- Median cadence: 5 posts per month.

## Decision
No evidence of saturation: cadence does not predict reach per post, across or within brands. Do not build a "post less"
recommendation on this sample. Null result, recorded.

## Follow-ups
- Audience-level saturation needs the brand's follower count over time, which we do not have.
- Same question on a monthly window and per platform with more posts per brand (Brand24 could provide).
