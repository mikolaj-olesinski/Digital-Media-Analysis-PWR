# E59 · Forecast horizon curve, and training on every pair of checks

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: how does the error of the E40 forecast grow with the gap between the first and the later check, and with the age at the first check? Do all (earlier, later) pairs of checks (3,218 instead of 956 first-last pairs) train a better forecaster?
- Hypothesis: error grows with the gap (> 7 days worst) and falls with the age of the first check; training on all pairs gains ≥ 0.01 on TikTok and X.

## Setup
- Snapshots from `history.snapshots`; every (earlier, later) pair with gap ≥ 1 h. Features as E40 (`history.features`). CatBoost on growth (E40), trained on first-last pairs only vs all pairs; persistence. 5-fold brand CV, evaluated on the first-last pairs.
- MAE by gap bin and by age-of-first-check bin.

## Result
3,218 pairs (TikTok 1,773 / X 1,156 / YouTube 289; YouTube has only first-last pairs). Evaluated on first-last pairs (336 / 289 / 331):

| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| persistence | 0.248 | 0.352 | 0.481 |
| CatBoost, first-last pairs (E40) | 0.164 | 0.188 | 0.275 |
| CatBoost, all pairs | 0.184 (-0.020, CI -0.032 to -0.008) | 0.188 (same data) | 0.267 (+0.008, -0.002-0.018) |

By gap (CatBoost first-last): TikTok < 12 h 0.098, 12-48 h 0.143, 2-7 d 0.152, 7-30 d 0.178; YouTube 7-30 d 0.212, > 30 d 0.156. By age at the first check: first check < 12 h is the hardest (TikTok 0.228, YouTube 0.428, X 0.283), a first check older than a week is easy (TikTok 0.145, YouTube 0.017-0.113).

## Decision
Hypothesis partly supported: on TikTok the error grows with the gap (0.10 → 0.18) and on all platforms it falls with the age of the first check (a check in the first 12 hours is 2-4× harder). Training on all pairs is worse on TikTok and not clearly better on X, so intermediate pairs do not add information; keep first-last training (E40).
