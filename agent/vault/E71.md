# E71 · Can the author's level be predicted without history?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: is the author's stable over-performance (E68) explained by what is known about the account without history, so it would work for new authors?
- Hypothesis: partly (Spearman ≥ 0.3) from followers, account kind, language, share of zero-reaction posts.
- Refuted if: Spearman < 0.1.

## Setup
- Authors with ≥ 2 posts with views (61 TikTok, 255 YouTube, 83 X). Target: mean over-performance of all their posts. Features: number of posts, log median followers, account kind (E66), main language, share of zero-reaction posts. CatBoost (depth 4), 5 folds grouped by the author's main brand. Ceiling: split-half Spearman (odd vs even posts).

## Result
| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spearman, features | -0.05 | -0.03 | -0.12 |
| R², features | -0.28 | -0.21 | -0.41 |
| split-half Spearman, history | 0.70 | 0.71 | 0.68 |
| sd of author level (log10) | 0.44 | 0.59 | 0.56 |

## Decision
Refuted: nothing we know about an account predicts its level, while its own history predicts it with rho 0.7. The author level is hidden information (audience outside followers, recommendation reach, possibly paid promotion), available only from the author's past views. New authors stay at the final model's error.
