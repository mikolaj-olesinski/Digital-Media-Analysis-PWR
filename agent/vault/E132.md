# E132 · FB CQR by tier

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the global FB band covers 0.925 (E123). Does one width cover small and large pages?
- Hypothesis: the largest posts are harder and under-covered.
- Refuted if: coverage is uniform.

## Setup
- CQR on the FB transfer (sources X + YouTube, calibration on 25% of FB brands), 5-fold brand CV, 402 posts.
- Coverage and width by follower tier and by quartile of measured views.
- Run: `python src/fb_cqr_tiers.py`.

## Result
- Overall: coverage **0.925**, median width 1.97.
- By followers: <1k 0.933 (w 2.84), 1k-10k 0.914 (2.45), 10k-100k 0.967 (2.14), 100k-1M 0.930 (1.95),
  **>1M 0.857 (1.61)**.
- By measured views: Q1 0.970 (2.68), Q2 1.000 (2.18), Q3 0.950 (1.72), **Q4 0.782 (1.49)**.

## Decision
The global band hides a real gap: the top quartile of FB posts and pages above 1M followers are under-covered (0.78 and
0.86). Calibrate the FB band by follower tier (known before the check) and do not report a single FB width. Widen for
large pages.

## Follow-ups
- Tier-specific CQR on FB by follower bin; the calibration sample per tier is small (15-90 posts), so pool bins.
