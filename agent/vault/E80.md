# E80 · Intervals for brand totals

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: how wide is an honest interval for a brand's total views (what a client sees), and does summing independent post errors give it?
- Hypothesis: brand-level quantiles are calibrated; independent post errors give intervals that are too narrow, because errors within a brand are correlated (authors).
- Refuted if: both are calibrated.

## Setup
- Brands with ≥ 5 posts with views; calibrated totals as in E73. A: total × 10^(5-95% or 10-90% quantiles of the brand log ratio on other folds); A by size: quantiles from brands of similar size (5-9, 10-29, 30+ posts). B: each post's prediction plus residuals drawn independently from other folds' posts, summed (500 draws). Coverage and median half-width on held-out brands.

## Result
- A (80%): coverage 0.79 / 0.80 / 0.80, half-width ×2.2 / ×3.0 / ×3.3; (90%): 0.88 / 0.90 / 0.89, ×2.9 / ×4.8 / ×4.2.
- A by size: about the same (0.77-0.81 at 80%).
- B (80%): 0.75 / 0.85 / 0.75; too narrow on TikTok and X, too wide on YouTube.

## Decision
Supported. Brand-level quantiles give calibrated intervals for brand totals: an 80% interval is about ×2.2 / ×3.0 / ×3.3 either way. Summing independent post errors is not calibrated.
