# E62 · Why did YouTube views per like fall in July-August 2026?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E46 found median log10(views per like) on YouTube falling from 1.57 (May) to 1.13 (August) while the error stayed flat. Is it because later posts were checked younger (less time to collect views)?
- Hypothesis: median age at the check falls by more than half from May to August on YouTube, and with the age column (E44) the monthly bias of the final model shrinks.

## Setup
- Posts 2026-04 … 08 per platform: posts, median age at the check, median log10 views per like, mean signed error of the final model with and without the age column (E44 out-of-fold predictions).

## Result
Median age of the post at the view check, by month of posting (2026-04 … 08): TikTok 93 → 8 days, YouTube 100 → 0.9 days, X 89 → 0.6 days. Median log10 views per like: YouTube 1.56 / 1.57 / 1.54 / 1.30 / 1.13, TikTok 1.37-1.40 then 1.34, X 1.15-1.34 without trend. Mean signed error of the final model by month stays within -0.11 … +0.03; with the age column (E44) within -0.09 … 0.00, without a clear shrinking.

## Decision
Hypothesis supported for the cause, not for the fix: later posts were checked much younger (on YouTube in August within a day of posting), so they had less time to collect views per like. The age column does not remove the monthly bias. TikTok and X were also checked younger without a fall in views per like, which fits the slower relative growth on TikTok (b = 0.35, E39) and that likes on X stop growing early. The drift is a property of the data collection, not of the platforms.
