# E41 · How good is the forecast without the early view count?

## Question and hypothesis
Early reactions alone carry most of the forecast signal.
Refuted (it was): the model without early views is much worse.

## Setup
Same CatBoost and folds as E40 with first-check likes, comments, shares, followers and ages only.

## Result
MAE 0.368 / 0.444 / 0.544; the early view count is worth +0.20 (0.17-0.24) / +0.26 (0.21-0.30) / +0.28 (0.23-0.32).

## Decision
On FB/IG, where views never appear, a forecast would be about as weak as here. Hypothesis refuted.

## Follow-ups
