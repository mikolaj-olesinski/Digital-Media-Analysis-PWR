# E79 · Live forecast plus author history

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: does the author's history improve the live forecast from the first check (E40)?
- Hypothesis: yes, as for the now-cast (E70).
- Refuted if: no gain.

## Setup
- E40 pairs (first check → later check) with out-of-fold CatBoost forecasts on the first check, reactions and ages. Correction from the author's earlier posts: shrunk sum of forecast residuals, or of now-cast residuals (E70).

## Result
- Only 31 forecast pairs (YouTube) have an earlier post by the same author; none on TikTok and X.
- YouTube: forecast residuals +0.005 (-0.036 to 0.041); now-cast residuals -0.060 (-0.101 to -0.019).

## Decision
Inconclusive: too few pairs. With a first check the forecast error is already small (0.10 on YouTube), so little is left for the author; now-cast residuals do not transfer to the forecast.

## Follow-ups
- Needs more checked posts by repeat authors (second measurement, B16).
