# E136 · Rolling-origin validation

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: is the brand-CV MAE representative of the future? For the last months with enough posts, train on everything
  before the month and test on the month.
- Hypothesis: rolling MAE close to brand CV, with some drift.
- Refuted if: the future is much worse.

## Setup
- Last 5 months with >= 150 posts; train on earlier months only; test on the month; final CatBoost features.
- Run: `python src/rolling_origin.py`.

## Result
| platform | windows | MAE mean ± sd | per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 3 (Jun-Aug) | **0.270 ± 0.004** | 0.274 / 0.265 / 0.270 |
| YouTube | 3 | **0.450 ± 0.018** | 0.470 / 0.454 / 0.427 |
| X | 2 (Jul-Aug) | **0.473 ± 0.005** | 0.468 / 0.478 |

- Brand CV for comparison: 0.273 / 0.456 / 0.467.

## Decision
The brand-CV number is representative of the next month: the rolling MAE is within 0.01 of it on average (YouTube
improves to 0.427 in August). This supports reporting one decimal and the claim that the validation is not
over-optimistic in time.
