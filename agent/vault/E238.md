# E238 · One grid: ridge in and out of platform, export labels and same-moment re-read

## Question and hypothesis
- Question (slides): are the in-platform and out-of-platform results comparable, and how much of the out-of-platform penalty on YouTube and X is the stale export?
- Hypothesis: with views and reactions read at the same moment, out of platform is about as good as in platform.

## Setup
- Ridge on log likes + log comments. In platform: brand-grouped 5-fold CV on the platform's own posts (export: all posts; re-read: the re-read posts, TikTok only 426). Out of platform: train on the other platforms (FB / IG: X + YT), numbers from the E201 / E210 grids.
- `src/reread_matrix.py` (seconds, local).

## Results (x10^MAE, TT / YT / X / FB / IG)
- In platform, export: x1.99 / x4.15 / x3.30; re-read: x2.06 (426 posts) / x2.10 / x2.96 / FB x2.42 / IG x2.41 (own platform, reads are always same-moment).
- Out of platform, export: x2.05 / x4.09 / x4.47 / x2.43 / x2.72; re-read: x2.29 (426 posts) / x2.14 / x3.01 / x2.44 / x2.41.

## Decision
Supported. With same-moment reads, out of platform costs almost nothing (YouTube x2.14 vs x2.10, X x3.01 vs x2.96); the large export gap on X (x4.47 vs x3.30) is mostly stale views. TikTok is the exception (export was fresh, re-read only 426 posts). Presentation only.
