# E187 · Negative posts (crisis monitoring)

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Is reach of negative posts under- or overestimated?
- Hypothesis: No bias on negative posts.

## Setup
- Sentiment part of E182 (Brand24 sentiment).
- Part of the critical review batch: `python src/critical_review.py` (via Kueue; E178 locally).

## Result
- Negative posts: TikTok bias +0.02 (115 posts), YouTube -0.04 (40), X +0.06 (CI -0.01 to +0.11, 478).
- The only significant sentiment bias is YouTube positive (+0.17).

## Decision
Supported. Crisis (negative) posts are not systematically mis-estimated; the model can be used for negative-mention reach with the usual bands.
