# E150 · A dedicated model for the largest FB pages

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the FB >1M tier is the hardest (E148). Does a model trained only on source posts from pages above 1M
  followers beat the global transfer?
- Hypothesis: yes, the relation differs at the top.
- Refuted if: no gain.

## Setup
- Ridge on likes + comments, trained either on all source posts or only on source posts from pages >1M followers;
  applied to the 70 FB posts above 1M followers.
- Run: `python src/fb_big_model.py`.

## Result
| training set | MAE >1M | bias |
|---|---|---|
| all sources | 0.410 | +0.33 |
| **big sources only** | **0.368** | **-0.06** |

## Decision
Training on large pages only improves the >1M FB MAE (0.410 -> 0.368) and removes the level bias. It is a small gain on
70 posts, but it is the right shape: the top of the distribution needs its own relation. Combine with the followers
feature (E149) and test whether the coverage improves (E151).
