# E84 · Country, local time, local holidays and industry as columns

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: do country (one-hot of the 10 most common), local time (main time zone of the country), local public holidays, the industry community (E69) or our type label add to the final model? The atlas (E83) showed country and hour effects.
- Hypothesis: country helps (US posts under-perform by ×0.8).
- Refuted if: gains with CIs crossing zero.

## Setup
- Final model + each group of columns, and + all four; out of fold on the same brand folds per platform; paired gain with brand-bootstrap CI. Local time is known for 91% of TikTok, 78% of YouTube and 21% of X posts (country missing otherwise). Holidays from the offline `holidays` package.

## Result
- Country: +0.005 (0.002-0.008) TikTok, 0.000 YouTube, -0.002 X.
- Local time and holidays: +0.002 / -0.002 / -0.004 (X CI below zero).
- Industry: -0.001 / -0.005 / -0.004 (YouTube CI below zero).
- Type label: +0.004 (0.002-0.007) / +0.005 (0.001-0.008) / +0.004 (CI crosses zero).
- All four: +0.007 / -0.005 / -0.001.

## Decision
Refuted. Country and the type label give at most +0.005, local time and industry nothing or worse. The country and hour effects in the atlas are already explained by reactions and followers, or too small to learn.
