# E46 · Rolling window: does the final model hold up month by month?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: all results so far use brand-grouped CV that mixes months. Brand24 would train on the past and predict new posts. Does the final model keep its error when it only sees earlier months, and does the error drift from month to month?
- Hypothesis: an expanding window (train on all months before M, test on month M; M = 2026-05 … 2026-08) gives about the same MAE as brand CV on the same posts (within ±0.03), because views per reaction should be stable over a few months. Training on earlier posts of the same brand may even help.
- Refuted if: the expanding window is worse than brand CV by more than 0.03 with a CI clear of zero on a platform, or the MAE rises month after month.

## Setup
- Data: TikTok / YouTube / X posts with views > 0, by `created_at` month. Almost all posts are 2026-04 … 2026-08; earlier months (2025-01 … 2026-03, 20-50 posts a month) go into the first training window only. 2026-09/10 (≤ 13 posts) are left out.
- Model: final model (`reaction_tiers.FINAL`, CatBoost MAE, 300 it.) on `extra_models.ENG`.
- Compared on the same test posts:
  1. brand CV: out-of-fold predictions of 5-fold brand-grouped CV on all months (the usual number);
  2. expanding: train on all posts before month M;
  3. expanding, new brands: as 2, but drop the test month's brands from training (time and brand both unseen);
  4. sliding: train on month M-1 only.
- Drift: median log10(views / (likes + 1)) and MAE of each scheme per month.
- Paired gains with brand bootstrap CI (`history.gain_ci`).

## Result
MAE per scheme on the same test posts (2026-05 … 08):

| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| posts | 1,949 | 2,000 | 1,685 |
| brand CV | 0.272 | 0.446 | 0.464 |
| expanding | 0.273 | 0.457 | 0.491 |
| expanding, new brands | 0.274 | 0.461 | 0.486 |
| previous month only | 0.285 | 0.469 | 0.496 |
| brand CV → expanding (CI) | -0.001 (-0.004-0.002) | -0.011 (-0.018 to -0.004) | -0.026 (-0.036 to -0.017) |
| brand CV → previous month (CI) | -0.013 (-0.019 to -0.007) | -0.023 (-0.032 to -0.015) | -0.032 (-0.042 to -0.022) |

By month, expanding vs brand CV: TikTok 0.280/0.272 (May), then within ±0.005; YouTube 0.485/0.459 (May), 0.468/0.464, 0.458/0.442, 0.427/0.427; X 0.552/0.480 (May, trained on about 100 X posts), 0.465/0.445, 0.466/0.459, 0.476/0.472.
Drift: median log10(views per like) on YouTube fell from 1.57 (May) to 1.13 (Aug); TikTok and X stable. The error did not rise with it.

## Decision
Hypothesis supported: no platform is worse by more than 0.03, and the error does not grow month by month. The gap is mostly the first test month, when the past is small (X had about 100 posts before May). Brand CV is therefore a fair estimate of what Brand24 would see once a few months of data exist. Brands already seen help little (new brands cost ≤ 0.005). Use all past data, not only the last month.
