# E124 · Final estimator v2: author_te + CQR

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: combine the two improvements (author target encoding, E119; conformalized quantile intervals, E118) into one
  estimator. Does it keep the point accuracy and the interval coverage, and where does it help?
- Hypothesis: a small MAE gain on authors with history, no loss elsewhere, CQR coverage about 90%.
- Refuted if: MAE rises or coverage falls.

## Setup
- Final CatBoost features + `author_te` (out-of-fold inside each training fold) for the point model.
- CQR with the same features (and with author_te) calibrated on 25% of the training brands.
- 5-fold brand CV, 251/251/251 split inside the training fold.
- Run: `python src/final_v2.py`.

## Result
| platform | MAE base | MAE +TE | coverage base | coverage +TE | width base | width +TE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.273 | 0.889 | 0.889 | 1.138 | 1.138 |
| YouTube | 0.460 | **0.457** | 0.902 | 0.902 | 1.706 | 1.720 |
| X | 0.470 | **0.466** | 0.891 | **0.897** | 2.070 | 2.082 |

## Decision
Use v2: CatBoost + author_te when the author has view history + CQR. The MAE gain is tiny overall (0.003-0.004) because
few authors repeat, but it costs nothing; CQR keeps 90% coverage. This is the estimator described in
`docs/en/model_card.md`.

## Follow-ups
- Author history is the lever: Brand24 has it even when the client does not.
