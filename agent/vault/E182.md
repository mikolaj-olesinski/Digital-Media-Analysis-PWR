# E182 · Error by language and sentiment

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Is the model biased for non-English posts (FB is 29% non-English vs 3-6% in training) or for negative / positive posts?
- Hypothesis: No subgroup bias beyond the CIs.

## Setup
- Out-of-fold final model (TikTok / YouTube / X) and the X + YouTube ridge on the 402 FB posts; MAE and bias (true - pred) per subgroup with a brand bootstrap CI (>= 30 posts).
- Part of the critical review batch: `python src/critical_review.py` (via Kueue; E178 locally).

## Result
| subgroup | posts | MAE | bias (CI) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FB English | 321 | 0.346 | +0.09 (-0.03 to +0.19) |
| **FB other language** (pl 43, es 17, fr 12) | 81 | **0.540** | **+0.35** (-0.04 to +0.57) |
| TikTok other language | 148 | 0.304 | +0.03 |
| YouTube other language | 74 | 0.549 | +0.17 (-0.04 to +0.30) |
| YouTube positive | 265 | 0.523 | **+0.17 (+0.04 to +0.29)** |
| YouTube negative | 40 | 0.458 | -0.04 |
| X negative | 478 | 0.473 | +0.06 (-0.01 to +0.11) |
| X neutral | 950 | 0.471 | +0.07 (+0.03 to +0.12) |

## Decision
Partly refuted. Non-English FB posts are underestimated about x2.2 (bias +0.35, CI touches 0 on 81 posts) and YouTube positive posts x1.5. Negative posts are not biased. Flag non-English FB estimates; treat the FB headline as English-dominated.
