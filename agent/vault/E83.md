# E83 · Residual atlas: who gets more views than reactions suggest

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: which post traits go with more (or fewer) views than the final model expects from reactions?
- Hypothesis: promo posts over-perform (E04); time of day and text style do not matter after reactions.
- Refuted if: time or style groups show differences with CIs away from zero.

## Setup
- Over-performance = true − out-of-fold final model (log10). Traits: language, country (top 6), weekend, hour (UTC, 4 bins), Brand24 sentiment, our type label, question mark, emoji, hashtags, text length, link, reply, words in capitals. Group mean minus the rest of the platform, brand-bootstrap 95% CI (400 draws), groups ≥ 30 posts; raw log-view difference alongside.

## Result
- Promo over-performs: +0.10 (0.07-0.13) TikTok, +0.06 (0.00-0.13) YouTube; 'unclear' posts under-perform (-0.09 / -0.12).
- US posts under-perform: -0.12 (-0.15 to -0.09) TikTok, -0.09 YouTube; Australia +0.10, India +0.12 on YouTube.
- X: 2+ words in capitals +0.17 (0.07-0.25); texts under 80 characters -0.15; Brand24 positive -0.10.
- YouTube: posted 12-17 UTC +0.10, 18-23 UTC -0.10; Brand24 positive +0.14; no hashtags -0.08.
- TikTok: question mark +0.07.

## Decision
Partly supported. Promo over-performance is confirmed; besides it, country (US below), hour on YouTube and text style on X move views by ×1.2-1.5 at equal reactions, CIs away from zero. Single comparisons out of about 100; read as descriptive, not causal.

## Follow-ups
- Country and hour could be tried as columns; earlier calendar and text groups gave at most +0.01 (E17-E21).
