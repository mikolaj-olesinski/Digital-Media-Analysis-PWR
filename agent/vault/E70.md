# E70 · Author history in depth, and at posting time

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E68 found that an author's earlier posts correct the final model by 0.10-0.16. Which form of the correction works best, does more history help, and does it work at posting time (no reactions)?
- Hypothesis: shrinking by the number of earlier posts is best; the gain at posting time is at least as large as with reactions.
- Refuted if: the posting-time gain is below 0.05 or its CI includes zero.

## Setup
- Posts with an earlier non-repeat post by the same author (other text, URL and view count): 63 TikTok, 379 YouTube, 112 X.
- Corrections to the out-of-fold final model: a × mean of earlier over-performance; a × last earlier post; empirical Bayes sum / (n + k). a and k chosen on the other brand folds. Split by the number of earlier posts.
- Same at posting time: the E43 model (followers + posting-time columns, no reactions), its out-of-fold over-performance, same corrections.
- Paired brand bootstrap CI.

## Result
| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| final model on these posts | 0.373 | 0.495 | 0.528 |
| + a × mean | 0.262 (+0.111) | 0.347 (+0.149) | 0.428 (+0.099) |
| + a × last post | 0.273 (+0.101) | 0.352 (+0.144) | 0.461 (+0.067, CI includes 0) |
| + sum / (n + k) | **0.258 (+0.116, 0.049-0.194)** | **0.347 (+0.149, 0.097-0.211)** | **0.426 (+0.102, 0.033-0.164)** |
| posting-time model | 0.655 | 0.632 | 0.582 |
| + sum / (n + k) | **0.486 (+0.169, 0.045-0.305)** | **0.491 (+0.141, 0.071-0.214)** | **0.433 (+0.148, 0.090-0.204)** |

- By number of earlier posts (shrunk correction, YouTube): 1 post 0.487 → 0.353 (239 posts), 2 posts 0.488 → 0.307 (65), 3+ 0.530 → 0.361 (75). One earlier post already gives most of the gain.

## Decision
Supported. The shrunk mean of all earlier posts is the best form (last post alone is weaker on X). At posting time the author's history gains +0.14 to +0.17: on YouTube and X, posting-time model + history (0.491 / 0.433) is as good as or better than the final model with reactions but without history on the same posts (0.495 / 0.528); on TikTok reactions still matter more (0.486 vs 0.373). One earlier post is enough for most of the gain.
