# E164 · Partial missingness crossover

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E163 showed that a fully missing feature should be dropped. At what share of missing values does retraining
  without the feature beat the full model?
- Hypothesis: a crossover around 40-50%.
- Refuted if: the full model always wins or the specialised model always wins.

## Setup
- Train full, followers-only and reactions-only models (5-fold brand CV); corrupt 0/25/50/75/100% of test posts on
  reactions or followers; compare MAE on all test posts.
- Run: `python src/partial_missing.py`.

## Result
Missing reactions (full -> followers-only):
| platform | 0% | 25% | 50% | 75% | 100% | crossover |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.491 | 0.719 | 0.929 | 1.139 | ~35-40% (specialised 0.583) |
| YouTube | 0.456 | 0.702 | 0.966 | 1.209 | 1.462 | ~33% (0.767) |
| X | 0.467 | 0.497 | 0.536 | 0.577 | 0.609 | ~70% (0.575) |

Missing followers (full -> reactions-only):
| platform | 0% | 25% | 50% | 75% | 100% | crossover |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.278 | 0.283 | 0.290 | 0.294 | ~0% (0.276) |
| YouTube | 0.456 | 0.522 | 0.582 | 0.640 | 0.711 | ~20% (0.494) |
| X | 0.467 | 0.533 | 0.596 | 0.657 | 0.733 | ~15-20% (0.515) |

## Decision
Add a switch rule: if more than ~35% of reactions are missing on TikTok/YouTube (or ~70% on X), use followers-only; if
more than ~20% of followers are missing on YouTube/X, use reactions-only; on TikTok the reactions-only model is already
as good as the full one without any missingness. This is a concrete product rule, not just a caveat.
