# E76 · Where the remaining error sits, and post and author traits as columns

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: which posts carry the error left by the final model, especially on X (replies, links, zero reactions, author activity, follower tier)? Do those traits help as columns?
- Hypothesis: the X error is concentrated in replies and a few very active authors.
- Refuted if: the error share of each group matches its share of posts.

## Setup
- Out-of-fold final model (5 brand folds per platform). Traits from text and the sample: reply (text starts with @), retweet, link, number of @mentions and hashtags, log number of the author's posts on the platform in the sample (with or without views). Share of posts vs share of summed absolute error per group; top 1/5/10 authors by posts. Final model + these 6 columns, paired gain with brand-bootstrap CI.

## Result
- X: replies are 43% of posts and 46% of the error (MAE 0.50 vs 0.44); zero-reaction posts 55% of posts, 58% of the error. The 10 most active authors carry 4% of posts and 5% of the error.
- YouTube: zero-reaction posts are 27% of posts and 40% of the error (MAE 0.66 vs 0.38).
- Traits as columns: +0.001 (-0.002 to 0.003) / +0.006 (0.001 to 0.012) / +0.011 (0.004 to 0.018).

## Decision
Mostly refuted. The error is spread over all posts, not concentrated in replies or a few authors; zero-reaction posts are the worst group (YouTube, X). Reply and author-activity columns give a small gain on X (+0.011) and YouTube (+0.006), CIs above zero.

## Follow-ups
- Could join the final model if the stack is rebuilt; gain is small.
