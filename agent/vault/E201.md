# E201 · Every combination of training platforms, tested on the platforms left out

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Which training platforms transfer best to which test platforms, with all five now having ground truth (Brand24 export TikTok / YouTube / X, FB 402 re-measured, IG 133 hand-batch reels)?
- Hypothesis: FB and IG are predicted about equally well from any mix; X and YouTube are the hard targets because their views per reaction differ.

## Setup
- Ridge on log likes + log comments (the fields all five platforms share), rows pooled as in E93. All 31 non-empty training sets; each platform not in training is scored. Reference: the same ridge in-platform with brand-grouped 5-fold CV.
- Sensitivity: each training platform weighted equally.
- Figure `figures/train_combos_{en,pl}.png` (training-set dot matrix + heatmap of 10^MAE). `python src/train_combos.py` (seconds, local).

## Result
| typical error x (10^MAE) | TikTok | YouTube | X | FB | IG |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| own platform (brand CV) | 2.0 | 4.1 | 3.3 | 2.4 | 2.4 |
| best single source | YT 2.0 | TT 4.1 | TT 4.1 | X+YT 2.4 (pair) / TT 2.6 | FB 2.4 |
| worst single source | X 3.4 | X 5.5 | YT 4.6 | X 3.1 | YT 2.8 |
| all four others | 2.1 | 4.1 | 4.4 | 2.5 | 2.6 |

- FB and IG: every training set gives x2.4-3.1, close to their own in-platform x2.4. FB from YouTube + X: x2.4 (= E93).
- X is the hardest target: x3.6-4.6 from others vs x3.3 own; YouTube x4.0-5.5 vs x4.1 own.
- Training on X alone is worst for every target (X has the lowest views per reaction).
- Equal platform weighting is worse everywhere (mean MAE e.g. FB 0.632 vs 0.482 pooled; FB from YT+X 0.599 vs 0.385): the mix of training rows sets the level.
- Brands: FB 20, IG 11 (preliminary). Same brands can appear on several platforms; posts never overlap.

## Decision
Supported. With reactions only, FB and IG transfer from almost any mix at nearly their own-platform error; adding platforms beyond two changes little. X is the platform that does not transfer in either direction. The pooling of training rows matters more than which platforms are included.
