# E53 · Fine-tuned transformer: the row as text, and the text as a correction to the final model

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: frozen embeddings added little next to reactions (E50). Does fine-tuning a multilingual transformer end to end help? Two ways, after the Kaggle "row as text" idea:
  1. **row as text**: the model reads "platform, followers, likes, comments, shares, text" as one string and predicts log10(views);
  2. **text correction**: the model reads platform + text and predicts the residual of the final CatBoost model (inner out-of-fold residuals, no leakage); the estimate is CatBoost + correction.
- Hypothesis: (1) alone is worse than CatBoost (numbers are read as tokens), but the mean of (1) and CatBoost gains ≤ 0.01; (2) gains ≤ 0.01, like the frozen embeddings. A gain ≥ 0.02 on any platform would refute "text adds little next to reactions" (E05, E50).

## Setup
- Model: `intfloat/multilingual-e5-small` (118 M parameters, word embeddings frozen), mean pooling + linear head, L1 loss on the platform-standardised target, AdamW (encoder 5e-5, head 1e-3), linear decay, 3 epochs, batch 32, 128 tokens, seed 0. CPU on homelab3, weights local, text never leaves the machine.
- One joint model for TikTok / YouTube / X; 5 folds grouped by brand over all three platforms; the base CatBoost (final model) uses the same fold of each post.
- Compared per platform: CatBoost; (1) alone; mean of CatBoost and (1); (2) CatBoost + correction; CatBoost + half the correction.
- Paired brand bootstrap CI (`history.gain_ci`). Out-of-fold predictions cached for E54.

## Result
Joint brand folds; CatBoost on the same folds: 0.271 / 0.453 / 0.467. Training time 3.4 h on 6 CPU (10 fine-tunes of about 20 min).

| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| row as text alone | 0.289 (-0.018, CI -0.025 to -0.012) | 0.454 (-0.001, -0.015-0.013) | 0.490 (-0.023, -0.036 to -0.010) |
| mean CatBoost + row as text | 0.272 (-0.001) | **0.434 (+0.019, 0.012-0.027)** | 0.466 (+0.001) |
| CatBoost + text correction | 0.275 (-0.005) | 0.444 (+0.009, 0.001-0.017) | 0.464 (+0.003) |
| CatBoost + half text correction | 0.263 (+0.007, 0.004-0.011) | 0.441 (+0.012, 0.008-0.016) | 0.458 (+0.008, 0.003-0.013) |

## Decision
Partly refuted, in the useful direction. A transformer reading the row as text (numbers as tokens) matches CatBoost on YouTube and is 0.02 worse on TikTok and X; averaged with CatBoost it gains +0.019 on YouTube, at the edge of the 0.02 bar. Text as a damped correction (half) is the only variant with a CI above zero on all three platforms (+0.007 / +0.012 / +0.008). So fine-tuned text does add something next to reactions, unlike frozen embeddings (E50), but no more than the CatBoost + MLP mean (E65) at 1,000× the compute.

## Follow-ups
Both variants enter the E54 stack.
