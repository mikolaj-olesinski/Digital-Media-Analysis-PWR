# E161 · Robustness to missing followers

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: followers are a core feature and often missing. How much does the MAE degrade when they are missing at
  prediction?
- Hypothesis: a moderate degradation, less than for missing reactions on TikTok.
- Refuted if: the model is insensitive.

## Setup
- Train on clean data (5-fold brand CV); set log(followers) to missing (flag 1) for a random 10/25/50/100% of test posts.
- Run: `python src/missing_followers.py`.

## Result
| platform | 0% | 10% | 25% | 50% | 100% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.277 | 0.278 | 0.283 | **0.294** |
| YouTube | 0.456 | 0.481 | 0.507 | 0.593 | **0.711** |
| X | 0.467 | 0.490 | 0.539 | 0.598 | **0.733** |

## Decision
The platforms are complementary: TikTok is robust to missing followers (0.273 -> 0.294) but fragile to missing
reactions (E138: 1.005); YouTube and X are the opposite (0.71-0.73 without followers, 0.93/0.61 without reactions).
Match the data-quality flag to the platform: TikTok needs reactions, YouTube/X need followers.
