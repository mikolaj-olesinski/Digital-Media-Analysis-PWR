# E133 · Recency weighting against drift

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the sample drifts in time (E46). Do exponential sample weights (half-life 90/180/365 days) help a model
  trained on older posts predict the newest 20%?
- Hypothesis: recency weighting improves the temporal test.
- Refuted if: no gain.

## Setup
- Train on the first 80% of posts by date, test on the newest 20%; weights 0.5^(age/half-life), half-life 90/180/365.
- Final CatBoost features, per platform.
- Run: `python src/recency_weights.py`.

## Result
| platform | test from | base | half 90 | half 180 | half 365 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 2026-07-24 | 0.274 | 0.273 | 0.273 | 0.272 |
| YouTube | 2026-08-05 | 0.440 | 0.442 | 0.445 | 0.439 |
| X | 2026-08-04 | 0.464 | 0.465 | 0.466 | 0.464 |

## Decision
No usable gain (at most 0.002, and some settings are worse). Do not add recency weights; the drift is small once
followers and reactions are known.
