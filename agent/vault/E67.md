# E67 · Topic clusters of all posts

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: do the 12,500 posts group into topics, and does the final model err systematically by topic?
- Hypothesis: clusters follow industries and languages more than brands; some topics (sport, giveaways) are biased by ≥ 0.1.
- Refuted if: no cluster with ≥ 40 posts with views has |bias| ≥ 0.1.

## Setup
- k-means (k = 16) on L2-normalised multilingual-e5-small embeddings of title + content (E50 cache); words per cluster by class-based TF-IDF (mentions, URLs and digits removed).
- Per cluster: posts, brands, share promo / spam (our labels), main platform, median views and bias of the out-of-fold final model on TikTok / YouTube / X posts. NMI with brand, platform, type.

## Result
- NMI with brand 0.30, platform 0.20, type 0.13 (E21 Louvain on text kNN: 0.49-0.65 with brand).
- Clusters: tech and phones; cars and dealers; sport events (Olympics, motor sport, NBA); jobs and campus hiring (72-84% promo, mostly Instagram / Facebook); luxury resale; service complaints (postal, airlines, telecom; 12% promo, X); food chains; and language clusters (Swedish, Polish, Thai / German / French, Vietnamese).
- Bias on clusters with ≥ 40 posts with views lies within ±0.1 overall except luxury resale (-0.24, 97 posts, ×1.7 underestimated); per platform up to -0.21 (luxury resale on TikTok, sport on X), -0.19 (fashion / foryou on YouTube), +0.29 (cluster 0 on X), -0.15 / -0.17 (tech on TikTok / X).
- Language clusters (Polish, Swedish) are almost only Facebook / Instagram: 5-14 posts with views.

## Decision
Supported, narrowly. The posts group by topic and language into readable clusters (a good map of the data for the paper); one topic (luxury resale) is underestimated by 0.24, the rest stay within ±0.1: reactions absorb most topic effects. Per-platform biases up to ±0.2-0.3 exist on clusters of 40-100 posts. Consistent with E50 (embeddings add ≤ 0.01 next to reactions).
