# E82 · Pre-registered estimates for the FB / IG hand check

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: what do our transfer models and Brand24 estimate for the 312 hand-check FB / IG videos and Reels, written down before any view is seen?
- Pre-registered expectations: (1) the best transfer has MAE 0.45-0.70 on FB / IG (E13 range on unseen platforms); (2) Brand24 reach is too high on FB (around ×10) and too low on IG; (3) ridge on likes + comments beats models with followers on FB (E81).
- Refuted if: the hand check shows otherwise; scored in the same notebook once filled in.

## Setup
- Models trained on all posts with views of TikTok / YouTube / X: ridge on likes + comments per source and on all three; CatBoost on likes + comments; CatBoost on reactions + followers (FB only). Inputs: reactions recorded by Brand24. Saved per mention id to `data/processed/fbig_preregistered.csv`. Scoring (`fbig_more.evaluate`): MAE, bias, Spearman, and MAE after a shift fitted on 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 posts; reactions at the check replace Brand24's. Pipeline tested on an invented fill in the scratchpad (not a result).

## Result
- Median estimates (log10 views): FB 2.30-2.39 for likes + comments models (about 200-250 views), 2.70 with followers; IG 1.53-1.98 (about 35-95 views).
- Brand24 reach: FB 3.58 (about 3,800, ×15-20 above ours), IG 1.30 (20, below ours).
- Implied views per like: ours 27-40 on FB and 30-42 on IG; Brand24 136 on FB (constant 100) and 20 on IG.
- Hand check: 0 rows filled at the time of writing.

## Update 2026-11-06 (after the hand check)

- The hand check was filled (130 FB posts with views; IG has no views, only likes/comments). Scored in E93/E94.
- FB transfer MAE **0.345** (CI 0.281-0.435) — better than the pre-registered 0.45-0.70, so expectation (1) was
  refuted in the good direction.
- The rule is too high on FB, but at **x4.1** on the 402 fetched posts (median), not x10-20; expectation (2) confirmed
  in direction, wrong in size.
- Ridge on likes + comments beats the run with followers (0.345 vs 0.627); expectation (3) confirmed.
- IG: no views, so the pre-registered IG estimate cannot be scored.

## Decision
Fulfilled: the pre-registration is closed by E93/E94. The main miss is the size of the FB rule error (x4, not x10-20);
the transfer was better than expected.
