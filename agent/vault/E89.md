# E89 · Few-shot recalibration with k target posts

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: with only k = 5-100 posts with true views from a new platform, what is the best correction of the out-of-platform ridge (literature scan: Kuzborskij & Orabona 2013, Daumé 2007, empirical Bayes)? And how good is a few-shot conformal interval?
- Hypothesis: a shrunk shift beats the plain median shift at k = 5-10; a slope helps only from k ≈ 50.
- Refuted if: the median shift is best at every k.

## Setup
- Leave one platform out, ridge on log likes + log comments trained on all posts of the two other platforms. k target posts drawn by brand (≤ 2 per brand), 200 draws, scored on target posts of other brands. Variants: median shift; mean shift; empirical-Bayes shrunk shift λ = k / (k + σ² / τ²) with τ² from the two source-to-source biases, or from all six platform pairs (what FB / IG would use; in this test it includes the target, so slightly optimistic); intercept + slope with slope shrunk to 1; ridge biased to the source weights (penalty by leave-one-out on the k posts). 90% interval: split conformal on the k posts after the shift vs exchangeable conformal from the sources.

## Result
- Zero-shot ridge 0.312 / 0.604 / 0.642. τ² from the two sources: 0.081 / 0.098 / 0.003 (X shift underestimated); from all six pairs 0.061.
- k = 5: median shift 0.347 / 0.679 / 0.621; EB with τ² from all pairs 0.315 / 0.626 / 0.588 (best or near best everywhere); EB with τ² from two sources fails on X (0.637).
- k = 25-100: on X intercept + slope or biased ridge 0.525-0.550 vs median shift 0.550-0.560; on TikTok biased ridge 0.304-0.313 at k ≥ 25; on YouTube nothing beats the zero-shot 0.604 by much (median shift 0.607-0.618).
- 90% interval: source conformal covers 0.99 / 0.79 / 0.81 (too wide on TikTok, too narrow on YouTube and X); few-shot conformal covers 0.87-0.92 from k = 10, half-width ×4-6 TikTok, ×14-18 X, ×28-49 YouTube.

## Decision
Supported. For FB / IG: with 5-10 hand-checked posts use the shift shrunk toward zero (τ² from all six platform pairs), not the raw median; from about 25-50 posts fit intercept + slope (biased ridge). A few-shot conformal interval is calibrated from about 10 posts but very wide on YouTube-like platforms.
