# E49 · Context from other posts of the same author, hashtag and brand (without views)

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Brand24 sees all mentions, also those without views (FB/IG). Do features from other posts of the same author, the same hashtags and the same brand help the final model? Unlike E07/E29/E43, these use no view counts at all (only likes and followers of other posts, leave-self-out), so test brands may use their own context without leakage.
- Hypothesis: "likes relative to the author's usual likes" and "likes relative to the brand's usual likes" add ≥ 0.01 on at least one platform; hashtags and copies add ≈ 0.
- Refuted if: every group's paired gain CI includes zero or is below 0.01.

## Setup
- Context computed on all 12,500 mentions (all five platforms), leave-self-out: author (posts, brands, mean log likes of their other posts, own likes minus that), hashtags (count, max frequency in the data, mean log likes of other posts with the tag), brand on the platform (posts, mean log likes and followers of other posts, own likes minus that, brand's mean likes on other platforms incl. FB/IG), copies of the same normalised text.
- Base: final model (CatBoost MAE, ENG). New: base + each group, base + all. Also a model without reactions (followers only) + all context.
- 5-fold brand-grouped CV, paired brand bootstrap CI (`history.gain_ci`).

## Result
Paired gain over the final model (MAE 0.273 / 0.456 / 0.466), brand bootstrap CI:

| added | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| author (posts, brands, mean likes of other posts, own likes vs that) | -0.001 (-0.003-0.002) | +0.003 (-0.001-0.009) | +0.003 (-0.001-0.008) |
| hashtags | -0.002 (-0.004 to -0.000) | -0.003 (-0.009-0.002) | -0.001 (-0.005-0.003) |
| brand (posts, mean likes/followers of other posts, own likes vs brand, other platforms) | -0.001 (-0.004-0.002) | **-0.014 (-0.019 to -0.008)** | -0.008 (-0.016-0.001) |
| copies of the same text | +0.001 | -0.003 (-0.005 to -0.000) | -0.001 |
| all context | -0.000 | -0.008 (-0.016 to -0.001) | -0.002 |
| without reactions: followers → + all context | +0.007 (-0.000-0.014) | **+0.054 (0.041-0.067)** | +0.009 (0.001-0.017) |

## Decision
Hypothesis refuted: no context group adds ≥ 0.01 next to reactions, and brand context makes YouTube worse (-0.014): brand averages from other posts let the trees split on the brand, which does not carry over to held-out brands. Author context is the closest to useful (+0.003 on YouTube and X, CI includes zero), but only 17% of mentions have an author with another post. Without reactions the same context is worth +0.054 on YouTube, about as much as text (E50). Joins the graph results (E03, E07, E29): what other posts say about a post adds little once its own reactions are known.

None for the now-cast. For FB/IG and posting time, context and text are the inputs that remain (see E50).
