# E197 · Third-party mentions at 60 brands, 203 videos

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: does the mention-population result keep improving with a larger brand frame?
- Hypothesis: low/mid around 0.55, top near 1.0, overall below 0.65.
- Refuted if: the larger sample degrades or the bias grows.

## Setup
- `src/external_youtube_mentions.py` with 60 brands; four mention-like queries each; brand channels excluded; 2025+.
- Model trained on Brand24 YouTube; scored with `src/external_validate.py`.

## Result
| quantity | E195 (40 brands) | **E197 (60 brands)** |
|---|---|---|
| videos | 144 | **203** |
| zero-view | 4 | **10** |
| below 100 / below 1,000 | 49 / 67 | **83 / 110** |
| median views | 2,151 | **437** |
| validation rows | 137 | **189** |
| MAE | 0.638 | **0.606** |
| bias | +0.351 | +0.278 |
| MAE low / mid / top | 0.558 / 0.553 / 1.047 | **0.536 / 0.555 / 1.044** |
| calibration slope | 1.215 | 1.171 |
| median baseline MAE | 1.726 | 1.640 |

## Decision
At 60 brands and 203 videos the picture is stable: **0.536 low / 0.555 mid / 1.044 top**, overall **0.606** (bias +0.28),
with 10 zero-view posts and 83 below 100 views. The mention population is now measured well; the only remaining boundary is
video size, and the top stratum (~1.0, ~x11 low) is the part that needs a tier-aware correction (E132, E115) rather than
more data.
