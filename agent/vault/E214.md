# E214 · Do synthetic large posts (SMOTER / SMOGN) fix the under-prediction of large posts?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: does oversampling the top decile of source views with synthetic posts reduce the bias on large posts without costing MAE?
- Hypothesis (falsifiable): the primary variant (SMOTER, 2x the rare set) reduces the top-tenth bias by true y by at least 0.1 log10 at a cost of at most 0.01 MAE, on at least 3 of the 5 targets (FB, IG transfer; TikTok / YouTube / X in-platform).
- Refuted if: it behaves like E57 / E180 (top-tenth bias moves by less than 0.1, or MAE worsens by more than 0.01) on 3 or more of the 5 targets.

## Setup
- Rarity: y above the p90 of the training fold. Synthesis: a rare post and one of its k = 5 nearest rare neighbours (standardised features + y), lambda ~ U(0, 1), interpolate features and y; missing flags from the anchor post. SMOGN: SMOTER + Gaussian noise sigma = 0.05 sd per column.
- Variants (all reported): SMOTER 1x / 2x (primary) / 5x; SMOGN 2x; SMOTER 2x + middle 50% undersampled to half.
- (a) Transfer: ridge on log likes + log comments, trained on re-read X + YouTube (drop rows with missing reactions; own loader copy in `src/wpb_common.py`) -> FB 402 / IG 642. Sensitivity: export X + YouTube source.
- (b) In-platform: `rt.FINAL` (CatBoost, MAE) on ENG, brand-grouped 5-fold CV, synthetic rows added to the training fold only.
- Base: the same model without augmentation, same folds. Paired brand bootstrap of the MAE gain (300 draws); bias in the top fifth / tenth by true y and by predicted y.
- Target views are never used for selection; all variants reported.
- Queue job: `amc-smoter`, 4 CPU / 4 GiB.

## Result
MAE of log10 views (typical error factor x10^MAE), brand bootstrap CI; gain = MAE(base) - MAE(variant), paired brand bootstrap, + = better. Bias = mean(y - pred) in the top tenth, + = under-predicted. Job `amc-smoter-1108-215542`, 4 CPU, about 1 min.

| target (posts) | base MAE | SMOTER 2x (primary) MAE | paired gain [CI] | top-tenth bias by true y, base -> new | top-tenth bias by predicted y, base -> new |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FB 402, re-read X+YT ridge | 0.387 (x2.44) [0.325, 0.457] | 0.406 (x2.55) | -0.019 [-0.029, -0.005] | +0.30 -> +0.20 | -0.11 -> -0.23 |

| TikTok 2,491, in-platform | 0.273 (x1.88) [0.263, 0.285] | 0.274 (x1.88) | -0.001 [-0.003, +0.002] | +0.25 -> +0.20 | +0.05 -> -0.01 |
| YouTube 2,441, in-platform | 0.456 (x2.85) [0.434, 0.480] | 0.462 (x2.90) | -0.006 [-0.012, -0.001] | +0.54 -> +0.43 | +0.04 -> -0.07 |

- Pre-registered rule (top-tenth bias by true y down >= 0.1 at <= 0.01 MAE cost): passes on IG, YouTube, X (3 of 5); fails on FB (MAE +0.019) and TikTok (bias -0.05 only).
- Other variants (all in the JSON): more oversampling moves the bias further and costs more MAE. SMOTER 5x: bias by true y down 0.08-0.25, MAE worse by 0.007-0.046 (CIs below zero on FB, TikTok, YouTube, X). Undersampling the middle costs 0.010-0.036. SMOGN 2x matches SMOTER 2x on the transfer and is close to the base in-platform (YouTube / X MAE -0.001 / -0.002, bias down 0.01 / 0.06). SMOTER 1x: MAE change +0.001 to -0.007, bias down 0.03-0.12.
- Bias by predicted y flips sign: posts the model ranks highest become over-predicted (X -0.13, FB -0.23). The augmentation stretches the upper end of the predictions. It does not rank large posts better: the true-y bias is mostly regression to the mean (E114).
- Export X + YouTube source (sensitivity): FB 0.385 -> 0.382 (gain +0.003 [-0.027, +0.030]); IG 0.435 (x2.72) -> 0.389 (x2.45), gain +0.046 [+0.028, +0.065]. With this source IG is under-predicted at every level (median bias +0.28), and oversampling high-y rows raises the level. The re-read source already removes that bias (IG 0.382), and SMOTER adds nothing on top of it.

## Decision
Formally supported on 3 of 5 targets, but it is a trade, not a gain. Top-tenth bias by true y drops by 0.10-0.15 at an MAE cost of 0.006-0.019 (CI below zero on FB, YouTube, X), and the top by predicted y becomes over-predicted. It is a stretch of the prediction scale, like the variance matching planned in E212, not new information. Keep it as a tail/totals option at most; the headline models stay unaugmented. The IG gain with the export source is a level correction that the re-read source already gives.
