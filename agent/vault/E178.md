# E178 · How much does the FB result depend on a few brands?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Is the FB transfer MAE (0.385 on 402 posts) carried by a few large brands?
- Hypothesis: The result is stable when any brand is left out.

## Setup
- Leave-one-brand-out MAE, MAE without the 3 largest brands, per-brand MAE (X + YouTube ridge on fresh likes + comments, E95 frame).
- Part of the critical review batch: `python src/critical_review.py` (via Kueue; E178 locally).

## Result
- 20 brands, 19 with >= 5 posts; the 3 largest brands hold 39% of the posts.
- Leave-one-brand-out MAE 0.363-0.414; **without the 3 largest brands 0.461** (vs 0.385).
- Per-brand MAE p10 / median / p90: 0.22 / 0.44 / 0.57.

## Decision
Partly supported. No single brand drives the result, but the three largest are easier than the rest: the typical brand is at 0.44-0.46, not 0.385. Report FB as "20 brands, typical brand ~0.45".
