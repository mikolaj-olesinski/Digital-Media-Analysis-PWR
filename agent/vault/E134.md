# E134 · Seed averaging

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the final model is a single CatBoost seed. Does averaging 5 seeds lower MAE?
- Hypothesis: variance reduction gives about 0.005.
- Refuted if: no gain.

## Setup
- CatBoost MAE, seeds 42-46, 5-fold brand CV; MAE of seed 42 vs the mean of 5.
- Run: `python src/seed_ensemble.py`.

## Result
| platform | single | ensemble | mean abs change |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.272 | 0.013 |
| YouTube | 0.456 | 0.454 | 0.022 |
| X | 0.467 | 0.468 | 0.015 |

## Decision
No usable gain (0.001-0.002, and X is worse). Keep one seed; the model is not seed-sensitive at the 0.01 level.
