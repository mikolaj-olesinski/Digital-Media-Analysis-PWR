# E115 · Calibration budget: how many target posts does a client need?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: plan B asks for the concrete "collect N posts and the error falls from A to B". For a new platform, how many
  posts with true views are needed before the transferred estimate is good?
- Hypothesis: a few posts help everywhere.
- Refuted if: the calibration does not lower the MAE, or only on some platforms.

## Setup
- Leave-one-platform-out ridge on likes + comments; for the target, a median shift fitted on n random target posts;
  MAE on the remaining target posts; 200 draws per n.
- Platforms: TikTok, YouTube, X (n up to 200); Facebook (n up to 100 of the 402 fetched posts).
- Run: `python src/calibration_curve.py`.

## Result
| target | n = 0 | 5 | 10 | 25 | 50 | 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | **0.313** | 0.347 | 0.331 | 0.321 | 0.317 | 0.315 |
| YouTube | **0.612** | 0.684 | 0.649 | 0.624 | 0.616 | 0.614 |
| X | 0.650 | 0.615 | 0.581 | **0.563** | **0.551** | 0.549 |
| Facebook | **0.385** | 0.426 | 0.402 | 0.390 | 0.385 | 0.382 |

- Calibration **hurts** at small n on TikTok, YouTube and Facebook (the shift adds noise to an already well-placed
  level) and only converges to the zero-shot value.
- On X it works: 25-50 posts cut the MAE from 0.650 to 0.551-0.563 (a real, large gain).

## Decision
The "collect N posts" recommendation is platform-specific: **X needs 25-50 posts; TikTok, YouTube and Facebook need
none** (zero-shot is already calibrated; checking a few posts can even hurt). This is the concrete answer to plan item B
and a useful sentence for the client: the budget is not universal, it is the price of a biased source (X).

## Follow-ups
- A slope (not only a shift) on X with 50 posts may be slightly better (E93/E97).
- For a new client platform with no source at all, the curve starts from the content/followers model (E43).
