# E103 · Cross-platform copies

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: is the same content posted on more than one platform in the sample, so that a platform multiplier could be
  estimated from repeated text (same content, different platform)?
- Hypothesis: campaigns repeat content, so some texts appear on 2+ platforms.
- Refuted if: such texts are essentially absent.

## Setup
- Title + content, lower-cased, URLs and punctuation removed, whitespace collapsed; texts longer than 30 characters.
- Count texts that appear on 2 or more platforms; 12 065 distinct texts.
- Run: `python src/xplat_copies.py`.

## Result
- Texts on 2+ platforms: **1 of 12 065** (0.008%, 6 rows, Facebook + Instagram).
- Within-platform copies are a different story (E63: 1-6% of posts).

## Decision
Cross-platform duplicates cannot be used to estimate a platform multiplier in this sample. The sample is brand mentions
around campaigns, not the same asset pushed everywhere. Null result; do not repeat without a different data source.
