# E227 · Red-team baselines: likes multiplier, median skill, paired CIs

## Question and hypothesis
- Question (preprint red-team): does our model beat a one-parameter likes multiplier (views = k x likes, k = median views/like of the training data) and the median, in platform and out of platform? Is "transfer = own-platform model" supported by a paired CI?
- Hypothesis: same platform, CatBoost beats the multiplier by >= 0.03 log10 on every platform; out of platform, ridge and the multiplier are within 0.03 (the transfer is mostly the views-per-like ratio). Refuted if the multiplier is worse than ridge by > 0.05 on FB and IG.

## Setup
- Same platform: TT / YT / X, 5-fold GroupKFold on E55 components (as results_a); training-fold median; likes multiplier fit on training-fold posts with >= 1 like, prediction log10(max(likes, 1)) + k; CatBoost from `results_preds.csv`.
- Out of platform: source = Brand24 export X + YouTube (results_b); targets FB 402, IG 642 (results_b.targets), Stack Exchange 538 (E225 primary mapping). Ridge (fe.ridge, fe.C2), likes multiplier, source median, target median (oracle, uses target labels), own-platform ridge in brand CV.
- Skill = 1 - MAE / MAE(median); brand-bootstrap 95% CIs (2000); paired transfer - own diff; per-brand MAE; IG / FB without the 3 largest brands.
- `src/baselines_check.py` (< 1 min, local).

The SE failure is the X source, not transfer itself. Spearman is the same for both (ordering comes from likes) except Stack Exchange (0.57 vs 0.48).

## Decision
Supported. Same platform CatBoost beats the likes multiplier everywhere (CIs exclude 0). Out of platform the transfer is essentially a calibrated views-per-like ratio: on FB ridge = multiplier = own-platform model (paired CIs include 0); on IG ridge is better than the multiplier (-0.034) but worse than the own-platform model (+0.054, CI excludes 0), so "almost as good as own platform" holds on FB only. Stack Exchange: the transfer is worse than a constant median (skill -0.71); even the own-platform ridge has skill 0.15 only. Preprint reworded accordingly; headline stays on the Brand24 export (the re-read IG gain of E210 was not blind).
