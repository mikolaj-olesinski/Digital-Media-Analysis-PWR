# E190 · Diverse external YouTube sample: small, large and zero-view videos

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: can we fetch a YouTube sample that spans the view distribution (small, large, zero) instead of only the
  search-popular videos, and how does the model do in each stratum?
- Hypothesis: zero-view public videos are not reachable; the model is calibrated for small posts and fails on large ones.
- Refuted if: zeros are reachable, or the model's error is flat across strata.

## Setup
- Four strata per brand (12 brands): most viewed from search, most recent from search, least viewed from the brand's
  channel, newest from the brand's channel; full metadata (views, likes, comments, followers, date).
- Fetch: `python src/external_youtube_diverse.py 12`; validate: `python src/external_validate.py <csv> <out>`.

## Result
| stratum | n | median views | zero-view | model MAE | bias |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| popular (search) | 36 | 1,544,176 | 0 | **1.480** | **+1.480** (x30 low) |
| recent (search) | 27 | 15,661 | 0 | 0.620 | +0.617 |
| newest (channel) | 32 | 3,943 | 0 | 0.601 | +0.563 |
| small (channel) | 36 | 876 | 0 | **0.289** | +0.167 |
| **all** | 131 | 11,489 | **0** | 0.761 | +0.717 |

- **Zero views are not reachable**: no public video with 0 views in 131 rows; the minimum is 71 views and only one is
  below 100. YouTube does not serve zero-view public videos through search or channel listings, so a "zero" in platform
  data means hidden/uncollected, not a real zero (consistent with E116).
- The sample spans 71 -> ~5M views, but 38 of 131 channel uploads are older than 2025 (dormant channels); the model uses
  the age, so they stay in.
- Calibration on the whole sample: slope 1.14, intercept 0.20; bias-corrected MAE 0.572.

## Decision
The model is a **small-post model**: on the small stratum it is as good as inside the export (MAE 0.289 against 0.456 CV,
bias +0.17), while on popular videos it under-estimates by ~x30 (MAE 1.48). This is not a bug but a calibration boundary:
the Brand24 mention population is small posts, and the model should be used there; for large brand videos a separate
recalibration or a tier-aware model is needed (E115, E132). The zero stratum cannot be sampled at all, which bounds any
claim about "zero-view posts".
