# E120 · Cross-platform author history

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: an author's posts on another platform may reveal their level even when the target platform has no history.
  Does adding the author's mean views on the other platforms help?
- Hypothesis: brand/creator accounts post on several platforms, so a gain is possible.
- Refuted if: authors almost never repeat across platforms, or the gain is zero.

## Setup
- Final CatBoost features + `author_other` = shrunk mean log views of the same author on the other two platforms
  (training rows only, test brands removed everywhere).
- 5-fold brand CV; MAE overall and on posts whose author appears on another platform.
- Run: `python src/cross_author.py`.

## Result
| platform | share seen on another platform | base MAE | +cross-author | seen subset base -> cross |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.0% | 0.273 | 0.273 | — |
| YouTube | 0.0% | 0.456 | 0.454 | 0.303 -> 0.322 |
| X | 0.1% | 0.467 | 0.467 | 0.517 -> 0.566 |

## Decision
Authors do not repeat across platforms in this sample (0-0.1% of posts), so a cross-platform author history cannot be
used; where it exists it is noise. This is consistent with E92 (brand effects do not carry across platforms) and E09
(reactions do not transfer). No follow-up without a data source that links accounts across platforms.
