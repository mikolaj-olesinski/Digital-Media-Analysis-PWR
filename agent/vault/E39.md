# E39 · How fast do views grow between checks?

## Question and hypothesis
Views follow a power law in post age, views ~ age^b, with b < 1 (slowing growth).
Refuted if: b ≥ 1 or the fit explains nothing.

## Setup
One pair per post (first and last check, gap ≥ 1 h): TikTok 336, YouTube 289, X 331. b from least squares through the origin of log growth on log age ratio; brand bootstrap CI (400 draws).

## Result
| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| b | 0.35 (0.29-0.39) | 0.58 (0.50-0.66) | 1.17 (0.76-1.93) |
| median growth | ×1.3 | ×1.2 | ×2.4 |
| first → last check (median) | 30 h → 222 h | 102 h → 706 h | 7.5 h → 14 h |

## Decision
Holds for TikTok and YouTube; not for X, which is observed only in its first day, where growth is still about linear in time. A view count on X taken at 7 h is far from later values.
