# E142 · Temporal CQR

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: are the 90% bands still calibrated when the test month is in the future?
- Hypothesis: coverage drops under temporal shift.
- Refuted if: coverage stays about 90%.

## Setup
- For the last 2-3 months with >= 150 posts: train on everything before the month, calibrate CQR on 25% of the training
  brands, test on the month; X uses log(age) (v3).
- Run: `python src/temporal_cqr.py`.

## Result
| platform | coverage per month | mean | width |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.914 / 0.893 / 0.915 | 0.907 | 1.14-1.22 |
| YouTube | 0.910 / 0.883 / 0.911 | 0.901 | 1.80-1.99 |
| X | 0.946 / 0.918 | 0.932 | 2.11-2.47 |

## Decision
The bands stay calibrated out of time (0.90-0.93); X is slightly conservative. Temporal validation supports reporting
the CQR band as the uncertainty, not only the random-CV one.
