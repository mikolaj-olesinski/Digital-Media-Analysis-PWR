# E135 · Follower-tier recalibration

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the model over-estimates small posts and under-estimates large ones (E18). Does a residual median per
  follower tier, fitted on calibration brands, fix that and lower MAE?
- Hypothesis: yes, at least on the large tier.
- Refuted if: MAE rises.

## Setup
- 5-fold brand CV with a 25% calibration split inside each training fold; residual median per follower tier
  (<1k, 1k-10k, 10k-100k, 100k-1M, >1M) applied to the held-out brands.
- Run: `python src/tier_recal.py`.

## Result
| platform | base MAE | recalibrated |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.276 |
| YouTube | 0.460 | 0.466 |
| X | 0.470 | 0.481 |

## Decision
Recalibration by follower tier makes MAE worse on every platform. The point model is already calibrated; the
over/under-estimation at the extremes is not a tier shift. Do not use. Consistent with E114 (reaction-tier shift
neutral, isotonic worse).
