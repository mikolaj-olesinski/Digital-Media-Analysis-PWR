# E97 · Which posts to hand-check

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: with a budget of k Facebook posts to check by hand, which selection removes the level error best?
  The current sample is stratified by reactions; is that the right design?
- Hypothesis: picking the extremes of the estimate, or points unlike the source platforms, beats random and beats
  stratification.
- Refuted if: all strategies are within noise of random.

## Setup
- Target: FB posts with views (130 sample and all 402); predictions of the E93 ridge (X + YouTube).
- Strategies, k posts: random; stratified by reactions (5 quintiles, the current design); extremes of the estimate
  (half lowest, half highest); brand-diverse (one post per brand); high-leverage (largest distance from the source
  mean in the two features).
- Calibration: one median shift fitted on the k posts, MAE on the rest; 300 random draws per cell.
- Run: `python src/active_check.py`.

## Result
| k | random | reactions | extremes | brands | leverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 (130) | 0.392 | 0.388 | 0.379 | 0.408 | **0.348** |
| 25 (130) | 0.350 | 0.350 | **0.334** | 0.341 | 0.382 |
| 50 (130) | 0.346 | 0.347 | **0.321** | 0.341 | 0.334 |
| 5 (402) | 0.426 | 0.422 | 0.389 | 0.442 | **0.385** |
| 25 (402) | **0.388** | 0.388 | 0.393 | 0.390 | 0.397 |
| 50 (402) | 0.385 | 0.384 | **0.377** | 0.391 | 0.405 |

Zero-shot: 0.345 (130) and 0.385 (402). The current reaction-stratified design is indistinguishable from random in
every cell. Extremes help at k = 50 by 0.025 (130) and 0.008 (402); leverage helps at k = 5. Differences of 0.01-0.03,
no CI computed, so **tentative**.

## Decision
Do not spend the hand-check budget on reaction-stratified sampling: it is no better than random. If only 5-10 posts
can be checked, take the extremes of the estimate (or the most atypical points); with 50, extremes still win.
The effect is small because the zero-shot level is already close (bias +0.06 on the 130, +0.14 on 402).
