# E166 · FB intervals on the 130 hand-checked posts

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the 402-post CQR covers 0.925 at width 1.99 (E123). Does the same procedure hold on the smaller 130-post hand
  sample, or is 130 too few for a calibrated band?
- Hypothesis: coverage close to 90%, width similar.
- Refuted if: coverage falls far below 90%.

## Setup
- FB hand sample (130 posts, subset of the 402); quantile LightGBM on X + YouTube; CQR calibrated on 25% of the brands;
  20 random splits.
- Run: `python src/fb_cqr_hand.py`.

## Result
- **130 posts**, 90% coverage **0.898**, width **1.59**.
- 402 posts (E123): coverage 0.925, width 1.99.

## Decision
The interval procedure holds on the smaller hand sample (0.898 at 90%) and the band is narrower because the hand sample
is slightly easier (E153). 130 posts are enough for a calibrated band on this sample; the 402-post number remains the
more representative one.
