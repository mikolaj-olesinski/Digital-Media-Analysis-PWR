# E220 · Can borrowed large-account data calibrate the FB band for pages above 1M followers?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the FB transfer 90% band under-covers pages above 1M followers (0.84, E145; a shift + 1.5x width reaches
  only 0.82, E147), because only about 70 FB posts are in that tier. Can residuals of re-read TikTok / YouTube / X posts
  from >1M-follower accounts fill the calibration pool?
- Hypothesis (falsifiable): adding those residuals, shifted to the FB level, to the >1M calibration pool lifts FB >1M
  coverage to at least 0.88.
- Refuted if: >1M coverage stays below 0.87, or the >1M band width grows more than 1.3x over the base.

## Setup
- E145 protocol: LightGBM quantile 0.05 / 0.95 (and 0.5 for the median) on export X + YouTube C2; conformity score
  max(L - y, y - H); 20 random splits with 25% of FB brands for calibration; tiers <10k / 10k-1M / >1M by export
  followers; Mondrian (per-tier) quantile with fallback to global under 20 calibration posts.
- Borrowed pool: re-read TikTok / YouTube / X (status ok, views present, likes and comments present, dedup by
  mention_id) with export followers >= 1M, scored with the same quantile models.
- **Primary (fixed before running):** >1M quantile from FB calibration >1M scores + borrowed scores, the borrowed y
  shifted by delta = median(y - M) of FB calibration >1M posts (all calibration posts if fewer than 5) minus
  median(y - M) of the borrowed posts. Delta uses calibration brands only.
- Variants, all reported: base global (E145 global), base per-tier (E145), borrowed unshifted, borrowed with delta from
  all FB calibration posts, borrowed only (no FB >1M scores). Coverage and mean width per tier.
- E150 point model on re-read sources: ridge on C2 trained on re-read X + YouTube (and TikTok + YouTube + X) posts
  from >1M accounts, scored on the FB >1M posts, vs the export X + YouTube all-source and big-source ridge; paired
  brand bootstrap.
- Run: `.venv/bin/python src/fb_big_borrow.py` (local, under a minute).

## Result
FB 402 posts with views, 20 brands; 296 have export followers (<10k 50, 10k-1M 176, >1M 70); >1M posts come from 11 brands,
about 15 of them in the calibration split. Borrowed pool: 167 re-read posts from >=1M accounts (YouTube 114, X 46,
TikTok 7). Median residual y - M: borrowed +0.30, FB >1M +0.30, so the FB shift delta is about 0 (median 0.004).
Coverage and mean width (log10) of the 90% band on held-out brands, mean of 20 splits, plain 0.9 quantile (E145):

| variant | <10k | 10k-1M | **>1M coverage** | >1M width (vs global) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| global (E145 base) | 0.913 | 0.902 | 0.836 | 1.55 (1.00) |
| per-tier, FB only (E145) | 0.913 | 0.874 | 0.830 | 1.58 (1.02) |
| **borrowed, delta from FB >1M cal (primary)** | 0.913 | 0.874 | **0.883** | 1.83 (**1.18**) |
| borrowed, delta from all FB cal | 0.913 | 0.874 | 0.867 | 1.59 (1.02) |
| borrowed, unshifted | 0.913 | 0.874 | 0.884 | 1.73 (1.11) |
| borrowed only (no FB >1M scores) | 0.913 | 0.874 | 0.877 | 1.79 (1.15) |

- Sensitivity with the finite-sample (n+1) quantile: base 0.859, primary 0.888 (width 1.07x), unshifted 0.891 (1.02x).
- The base reproduces E145 (0.836 vs 0.843 global, 0.830 vs 0.832 per-tier).
- The 10k-1M tier uses its per-tier quantile in all non-global variants (0.874); with the global quantile it is 0.902.
- No CI: each split scores about 55 held-out >1M posts from about 8 brands; the 20 splits overlap.

E150 point model on the 70 FB >1M posts (ridge on C2; paired diff vs global, brand bootstrap 300):

| source | MAE (factor) | mean bias | paired diff |
|---|---|---|---|
| export X + YouTube, all pages (global) | 0.410 (x2.57) [0.302, 0.566] | +0.33 | - |
| export X + YouTube, >=1M pages (E150) | 0.368 (x2.33) | -0.06 | -0.042 [-0.177, +0.080] |
| re-read X + YouTube, >=1M pages | 0.412 (x2.58) | -0.07 | +0.003 [-0.115, +0.114] |
| re-read TikTok + YouTube + X, >=1M pages | 0.402 (x2.52) | -0.06 | -0.008 [-0.121, +0.103] |
| re-read X + YouTube, all pages | 0.380 (x2.40) | +0.12 | -0.030 [-0.099, +0.039] |

## Decision
Supported, narrowly. Borrowing re-read >1M residuals lifts FB >1M coverage from 0.836 to 0.883 (just above the 0.88
bar) at 1.18x width; the variants land at 0.867-0.884. The large-account under-prediction is the same on TikTok /
YouTube / X as on FB (median residual +0.30 in both), so the shift is not needed and the unshifted pool does as well
(0.884 at 1.11x). This is the first variant that brings the >1M band near 90%, but it rests on 11 FB brands and has no
CI. The parent may offer it as the >1M band option (pooled cross-platform calibration, as E145 suggested); the point
model does not improve with re-read sources (all CIs include 0).
