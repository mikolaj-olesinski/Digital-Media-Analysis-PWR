# E63 · Copy-paste campaigns: copied texts, their views and their share of estimated reach

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: identical texts posted many times (E08: 6-14% near-duplicates; spam on X) — do they get fewer views per reaction, does the final model err on them, and how much of a brand's Brand24 reach comes from them?
- Hypothesis: copied posts have lower views per like and are overestimated by the final model; on X and FB/IG some brands get > 50% of their estimated reach from copies.

## Setup
- Copy = the normalised text (lower case, links and punctuation removed, > 20 characters) appears more than once in the 12,500 mentions.
- Per platform: posts, median views, views per like, reactions, followers, share of the Brand24 reach column; brands with > 50% of reach from copies. Final model MAE and bias on copied vs unique posts (out of fold).

## Result
Copied posts (same normalised text appears again): TikTok 30, YouTube 33, X 96, FB 159, IG 145 of 2,500 each (1-6%).

| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| median log10 views per like, copied vs unique | 1.62 vs 1.38 | 1.64 vs 1.38 | 1.39 vs 1.26 |
| share of the platform's Brand24 reach from copies | 0.8% | 12.0% | 1.8% |
| brands with copies / with > 50% of reach from copies | 14 / 2 | 15 / 6 | 29 / 7 |
| final model MAE, copied vs unique | 0.577 vs 0.270 | 0.742 vs 0.452 | 0.548 vs 0.463 |
| bias (estimate − truth), copied | -0.05 | **-0.26** | -0.14 |

FB 7.5%, IG 0.1% of reach from copies.

## Decision
Hypothesis refuted in direction: copied posts have more, not fewer, views per like and the final model underestimates them (most on YouTube, -0.26). They are rare (1-6% of posts) and inflate the total reach of the platform little, but for 6 YouTube and 7 X brands more than half of the estimated reach comes from copies, so per-brand reports can be dominated by one repeated text. The model is about 2× less accurate on copies.

## Follow-ups
Flag copied posts in brand reports; a copy-count feature was neutral in E49.
