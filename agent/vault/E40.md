# E40 · Can later views be forecast from the first check?

## Question and hypothesis
A model on first-check views, reactions and ages beats both persistence and a power law in age.
Refuted if: the gain over the power law has a CI including zero.

## Setup
Same pairs as E39. Bases: persistence; × median growth; × power law (fitted on training brands). New: CatBoost (MAE loss, 600 iterations, depth 4) on log growth with log views, likes, comments, shares at the first check, log ages, log followers. 5-fold brand-grouped CV; paired gain with brand bootstrap CI.

## Result
| MAE | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| persistence | 0.248 | 0.352 | 0.481 |
| power law | 0.194 | 0.233 | 0.345 |
| CatBoost | 0.165 | 0.188 | 0.269 |
| gain over power law | +0.029 (0.013-0.046) | +0.045 (0.014-0.077) | +0.076 (0.020-0.163) |

## Decision
Gain on all three. Tentative: one month, about 300 posts per platform, X only in the first day. Forecasting is feasible when an early view count exists.
