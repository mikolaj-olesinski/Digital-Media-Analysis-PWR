# E52 · Estimate at posting time with sentence embeddings and context from other posts

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the posting-time model (E43: followers, text stats, VADER, TF-IDF SVD 30, AI labels, calendar, horizon, brand size) gets 0.575 / 0.684 / 0.550. Without reactions, embeddings and context from other posts were worth up to +0.05 on YouTube over followers alone (E49, E50). Do they add on top of the E43 columns?
- Hypothesis: embeddings + context add ≥ 0.02 on YouTube and ≤ 0.01 on TikTok and X; replacing TF-IDF by embeddings changes little (both gave +0.051 in E50).
- Refuted if: the YouTube gain CI includes zero or is below 0.02.

## Setup
- Data and folds as E43 (`history.posting_time_data`, 5-fold brand-grouped CV, CatBoost MAE 600 it. depth 4).
- Base: E43 "posting-time columns". New: + e5 embeddings (PCA 16); TF-IDF replaced by embeddings; + context (E49 groups without the post's own likes: author posts, brands and mean likes of other posts; hashtag frequency and mean likes of other posts with the tag; brand posts, mean likes and followers of other posts, brand likes on other platforms; text copies); + both.
- Caveat: context uses likes of the author's and brand's other posts in the whole data, also later ones; at real posting time only earlier posts are known, so this is an upper bound.
- Paired brand bootstrap CI (`history.gain_ci`).

## Result
| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| E43 posting-time columns (MAE) | 0.575 | 0.684 | 0.550 |
| + embeddings | +0.005 (0.001-0.009) | +0.006 (-0.000-0.012) | +0.006 (0.000-0.012) |
| TF-IDF replaced by embeddings | +0.009 (0.003-0.016) | -0.005 (-0.016-0.006) | +0.008 (-0.001-0.018) |
| + context | +0.004 (-0.000-0.009) | **+0.020 (0.011-0.027)** | +0.008 (0.003-0.014) |
| + embeddings + context | +0.008 (0.003-0.013) | +0.017 (0.008-0.027) | +0.010 (0.003-0.017) |
| best MAE | 0.566 | 0.664 | 0.540 |

## Decision
Partly supported: context from other posts gives +0.020 on YouTube (CI above zero) and +0.004-0.008 elsewhere; embeddings add ≤ 0.009, mostly when they replace TF-IDF on TikTok and X. Embeddings + context together +0.017 on YouTube, just below the 0.02 bar. The posting-time estimate improves to 0.566 / 0.664 / 0.540 (×3.7 / ×4.6 / ×3.5), but reactions remain the main signal (0.27 / 0.45 / 0.47 with them). The context gain is an upper bound (later posts of the author and brand are used).

## Follow-ups
A strictly time-ordered context (only earlier posts) would give the deployable number.
