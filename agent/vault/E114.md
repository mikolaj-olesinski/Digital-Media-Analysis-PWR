# E114 · Validation audit and post-hoc recalibration

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: is the final model's headline MAE stable across splits? Is it calibrated? Does the recalibration listed in
  the plan (G: "post-hoc recalibration of predictions") improve it?
- Hypothesis: the single 5-fold number hides variance; recalibration will trim the systematic bias at the extremes.
- Refuted if: repeated CV is tight and recalibration does not lower MAE.

## Setup
- Final model: CatBoost MAE (300 iterations) on followers, likes, comments, shares and missing flags.
- Repeated grouped 80/20 splits: 5 seeds x 5 folds, MAE per draw.
- Out-of-fold predictions (5-fold brand CV); isotonic recalibration fitted on the training folds; a per-reaction-tier
  median shift fitted on the training folds.
- Bias by decile of the prediction.
- Run: `python src/validation_audit.py`.

## Result
| platform | repeated CV mean ± sd | OOF MAE | isotonic | tier shift | bias by decile |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.269 ± 0.011 | 0.273 | 0.279 | 0.274 | -0.11 (lowest) to +0.07 |
| YouTube | 0.466 ± 0.030 | 0.456 | 0.465 | 0.457 | -0.04 to +0.11 |
| X | 0.463 ± 0.018 | 0.467 | 0.476 | 0.469 | -0.03 to +0.11 |

- The headline is stable (YouTube has the widest spread, ±0.03).
- Isotonic recalibration **raises** MAE on every platform; the per-tier shift is neutral.
- Residual mean +0.02 / +0.04 / +0.04; residual sd 0.38 / 0.62 / 0.60.

## Decision
The point model is already calibrated in the middle; the plan's post-hoc recalibration does not help and should not be
added. The tail problem (E18) is not a monotone-map problem: it needs a different loss or a tail model, not a
recalibration of the point estimate. Validation is stable enough to report one decimal.

## Follow-ups
- Tail-specific loss (quantile 10/90 or asymmetric) instead of isotonic.
- The same audit for the interval, not the point.
