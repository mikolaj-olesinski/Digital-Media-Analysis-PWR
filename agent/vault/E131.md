# E131 · Out-of-platform CQR matrix

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: if the target platform has no training data, how wide is an honest 90% band? Train on the other two
  platforms, calibrate CQR on a quarter of the target brands, evaluate on the held-out target brands.
- Hypothesis: coverage about 90% but a much wider band than in-platform.
- Refuted if: the band is close to the in-platform one or coverage fails.

## Setup
- Quantile LightGBM 5/50/95 on likes + comments + platform code of the two source platforms.
- CQR calibration on 25% of the target brands; 5-fold brand CV on the target.
- Run: `python src/transfer_cqr.py`.

## Result
| target | sources | MAE | 90% coverage | width | in-platform width (E118) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | YouTube + X | 0.351 | 0.905 | 1.51 | 1.14 |
| YouTube | TikTok + X | 0.658 | 0.912 | 2.84 | 1.71 |
| X | TikTok + YouTube | 0.716 | 0.908 | 3.69 | 2.07 |

- Widths in log10: x32 / x690 / x4 900.

## Decision
Transfer is possible with an honest band: coverage stays at 90% after calibration, but the band is 1.3x (TikTok) to
1.8x (X) wider than in-platform, and the MAE rises by 0.08-0.25. Use transfer only with the wide band, and prefer
in-platform data when it exists. This is the quantitative "cost of no target data".
