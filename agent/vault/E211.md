# E211 · Is the E210 gain just post age?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E210 (re-read X + YouTube as the transfer source) cut IG MAE by 0.053. The re-read views are read at an
  age of months, like the FB / IG reads, while the export views were read at a median of ~4 weeks. Is the gain an age
  effect that a growth correction of the export labels would reproduce?
- Hypothesis (falsifiable): the re-read helps because its read age matches the FB / IG read age.
- Refuted if: export targets moved to the re-read age reproduce less than half of E210's IG gain (gain < 0.027,
  primary variant).

## Setup
- Pairs: posts with an export view and an ok re-read (X, YouTube, TikTok). Export age = metrics_updated_at -
  created_at, re-read age = fetched_at - created_at (days, clipped at 1).
- Growth exponent per platform: Δlog10 views = b · Δlog10 age, OLS through the origin (pure age model), estimated
  only on the source pairs. Never estimated from FB / IG.
- **Primary (fixed before running): every export X + YouTube post moved to its age at the re-read date (the
  platform's median re-read fetch time), y' = y + b · (log10 age_reread - log10 age_export); export reactions kept;
  ridge on C2 as in E210; scored on FB / IG.** Gain is compared with E210's IG gain of 0.053.
- Variants, all reported: b with an intercept (OLS; the intercept is a level shift, not age); also moving likes /
  comments with their own exponent; decomposition on the paired posts only (export y + export reactions -> moved y ->
  re-read y -> re-read y + re-read reactions).
- Descriptive: median log10(views / (likes + 1)) by age bin on export and re-read sources, and FB / IG read age.
- Paired brand bootstrap of the MAE difference vs the export base (2,000 draws).
- Run: `.venv/bin/python src/reread_age.py` (seconds, local).

## Result
Read age (median days): export X + YT 23.5, re-read X + YT 137, FB 310, IG 334.
Growth exponents from source pairs only (Δlog10 views per Δlog10 age, through origin; pairs): TikTok 0.18 (426),
X 0.25 (1,508), YouTube 0.56 (1,371). With an intercept X's slope is -0.02 and the intercept +0.40: X's export/re-read
gap is a level offset (x2.5), not growth with age. Likes exponents: TikTok 0.30, X 0.13, YouTube 1.01.

MAE (factor), paired diff vs the export base (2,000 brand draws, negative = better); FB 402, IG 642:

| source | FB | FB diff | IG | IG diff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| export X + YT (base) | 0.385 x2.43 | - | 0.435 x2.72 | - |
| **views moved to re-read age (primary)** | 0.485 x3.05 | +0.100 (+0.006, +0.183) | **0.402 x2.52** | **-0.033 (-0.111, +0.028)** |
| moved, b with intercept | 0.478 x3.00 | +0.092 | 0.404 x2.53 | -0.031 (-0.108, +0.029) |
| views + likes + comments moved | 0.388 x2.44 | +0.002 (-0.030, +0.039) | 0.385 x2.43 | -0.050 (-0.074, -0.028) |
| paired posts: export y, export reactions | 0.394 | +0.009 | 0.430 | -0.005 |
| paired posts: moved y, export reactions | 0.476 | +0.091 | 0.407 | -0.028 |
| paired posts: re-read y, export reactions | 0.454 | +0.069 | 0.401 | -0.033 |
| paired posts: re-read y, re-read reactions | 0.390 | +0.004 | 0.379 | -0.056 (-0.091, -0.028) |

- Primary IG gain = 0.62 of E210's 0.053, but its CI includes 0 and FB gets worse by 0.10: moving only the views
  over-shoots (median bias FB -0.35, IG -0.19).
- Views per like (median log10(views / (likes+1))) on export YouTube rise with age, 1.08 (<7 days) -> 1.55 (30-180 d);
  re-read X is 1.49-1.57 vs export X 1.23-1.30 at every age.
- The 2,879 paired posts with both reads re-read (y and reactions) reproduce the full E210 gain (IG 0.379); the 505
  new X posts are not needed for it.

## Decision
Not refuted by the pre-registered rule (0.62 of the gain >= 0.5), but the age-only story is incomplete. Moving only
the views to the later age reproduces the IG level (CI includes 0) and breaks FB (+0.10). What reproduces E210 on both
platforms is views and reactions at the same, later age (moved together: IG -0.050, FB +0.002), or simply the re-read
of both. The gain is a read-moment consistency effect: the ratio of views to reactions that FB / IG show at months of
age, which a month-old export does not have. For the paper: "train on views and reactions read together, at an age
similar to the target's"; an age correction of views alone is not a substitute.
