# E54 · Stack of every model family on the same folds

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: CatBoost, TabPFN, TabICL, CatBoost with the age of the check (E44), CatBoost with embeddings (E50) and the fine-tuned transformer (E53) each gain ≤ 0.01. Do they add up when combined with weights learned on held-out brands (as in the Kaggle stack)?
- Hypothesis: a convex stack, weights fitted per platform by nested brand CV, beats the final model by 0.01-0.02 on every platform and beats the plain mean of all members by ≤ 0.005.
- Refuted if: the stack gain over the best single member is below 0.005 with a CI including zero.

## Setup
- Joint 5 brand-grouped folds over TikTok / YouTube / X (the E53 folds); every member out of fold on them.
- Members: CatBoost final model; + log age of the check; + e5 embeddings (PCA 16); TabPFN v2 (median, 8 estimators, 3 seeds); TabICL (8 estimators, 3 seeds); MLP on ENG + embeddings (E65, 3 seeds); fine-tuned row as text; CatBoost + fine-tuned text correction.
- Stack: non-negative weights summing to 1, minimising MAE, fitted on 4 folds of the out-of-fold matrix and applied to the 5th (nested, so no weight sees its test posts). Also the plain mean of all members and of the best three.
- Paired brand bootstrap CI against the final model and against the best single member.

## Result
Joint brand folds, MAE TikTok / YouTube / X. Best single member: TabPFN v2 (TikTok, YouTube), CatBoost + age of check (X).

| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| CatBoost (final model) | 0.271 | 0.453 | 0.467 |
| best single member | 0.268 | 0.443 | 0.452 |
| mean of all 8 members | 0.262 | 0.430 | 0.447 |
| mean of the best three | 0.266 | 0.432 | 0.446 |
| **stack (nested weights)** | **0.260** | **0.425** | **0.441** |

- Stack vs final model: +0.011 (0.008-0.014) / +0.028 (0.021-0.036) / +0.026 (0.019-0.032).
- Stack vs best single member: +0.008 (0.005-0.011) / +0.018 (0.011-0.026) / +0.012 (0.005-0.018).
- Plain mean of all members vs final: +0.009 / +0.024 / +0.019; the learned weights add 0.002 / 0.005 / 0.006 on top.
- Mean weights: TikTok TabPFN 0.42 + text correction 0.38 + MLP 0.10; YouTube row as text 0.32 + text correction 0.26 + MLP 0.23 + TabICL 0.10; X age of check 0.51 + MLP 0.35 + text correction 0.12. The plain CatBoost gets weight 0 everywhere (its information is in the CatBoost variants).

## Decision
Supported. The small gains do add up: the stack beats the final model by 0.011 / 0.028 / 0.026 with all CIs above zero, and beats the best single member by 0.008-0.018. Most of it comes from plain averaging of diverse members (the mean of all eight already gives +0.009 / +0.024 / +0.019); learned weights add ≤ 0.006. Members that are weak alone (fine-tuned row as text, MLP) get large weights because their errors differ from CatBoost's. The gain is still small next to the noise floor (E56: 0.22 / 0.39 / 0.33), so the final model stays the single CatBoost for simplicity; the stack is the best accuracy we report.

## Follow-ups
- If accuracy matters more than simplicity: the mean of CatBoost + age of check, TabPFN, MLP and the text correction is a cheap near-stack (no weights to fit); costs a 3.4 h fine-tune and TabPFN inference.
- No further model families planned; remaining error is mostly irreducible (E56).
