# E147 · Can the >1M FB tier be calibrated?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: widening does not help (E146), which suggests a level bias. Does a calibrated shift for the >1M tier fix
  the coverage, with or without extra width?
- Hypothesis: the shift fixes most of the gap.
- Refuted if: coverage stays below 90%.

## Setup
- FB transfer; 20 random 75/25 brand splits; on the calibration split fit q_global and the median residual of >1M
  posts; apply the shift, and shift + 1.5x width, to the held-out >1M posts.
- Run: `python src/fb_bigfix.py`.

## Result
- Raw coverage **0.843**.
- Shift only: **0.780**.
- Shift + 1.5x width: **0.818**.
- Median shift: **+0.32** (the model under-estimates the largest pages).

## Decision
Even a calibrated level shift plus extra width reaches only 0.818: the 70 posts above 1M followers cannot be calibrated
with this sample. Report the >1M FB band as uncalibrated and wide (or do not report a band for that tier), and say the
under-estimate is about x2. Do not claim a 90% band there.
