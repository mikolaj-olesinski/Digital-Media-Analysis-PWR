# E141 · Final X model v3 with log(age)

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E139 found log(age) gives X -0.013. Does the gain survive the CQR calibration, and does the band change?
- Hypothesis: MAE falls and the band narrows slightly.
- Refuted if: coverage falls or the band widens.

## Setup
- X only: final features + log(age); CatBoost MAE for the point, LightGBM 5/95 + CQR for the bands; 5-fold brand CV,
  25% calibration split.
- Run: `python src/final_v3_x.py`.

## Result
- MAE **0.454** (was 0.467 in E114/E124).
- Coverage: 50% **0.502**, 90% **0.899**.
- Widths: 50% **0.83** (was 0.88), 90% **1.99** (was 2.07-2.08).

## Decision
Use v3 for X: adding log(age) lowers MAE by 0.013 and narrows both bands, with coverage intact. The model card X row is
updated. TikTok and YouTube stay on v2 (age is neutral there).
