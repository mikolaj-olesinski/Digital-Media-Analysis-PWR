# E170 · Brand news attention on Wikipedia at post time

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: does a spike in the brand's daily Wikipedia pageviews at the post's date (a news moment) explain views beyond reactions?
- Hypothesis: posts made while the brand is in the news over-perform, so attention relative to the brand's own baseline helps.
- Refuted if: paired gains with CIs at or below zero on TikTok / YouTube / X and no gain on the Facebook transfer.

## Setup
- `src/fetch_wikimedia_more.py daily`: daily English Wikipedia pageviews (user agents) 2024-01-01..2026-10-31 for the 293
  brand articles kept after E86's name check and hand review; only article titles were sent (user consent 2026-11-06).
- Columns (past and same day only, so they exist at prediction time): log10 views on the post day, the 7-day mean and the
  7-day peak, each minus log10 of the brand's median over the previous 60 days; -1 when missing. Coverage 83-86% of posts.
- Evaluation: final CatBoost (`em.ENG`) vs + columns, same brand folds, paired brand-bootstrap gain (TikTok / YouTube / X);
  Facebook: ridge on likes + comments (+ columns) trained on X + YouTube and scored on the 402 fetched FB posts, plus
  in-platform brand CV on those posts.
- Run: `.venv/bin/python src/fetch_wikimedia_more.py` (fetch), then `python src/external_brand.py` via Kueue.

## Result
| platform | final model | + attention | gain (95% CI) | Spearman column vs residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.274 | -0.000 (-0.003 to 0.002) | 0.02-0.04 |
| YouTube | 0.456 | 0.467 | **-0.011** (-0.016 to -0.007) | -0.01-0.03 |
| X | 0.467 | 0.474 | **-0.007** (-0.012 to -0.002) | -0.03-(-0.02) |

- Attention spikes are small: median 0.00, p90 +0.09, p99 +0.41 log10 over the baseline.
- Facebook transfer MAE 0.385 → 0.384 (in-platform CV 0.384 → 0.393).

## Decision
Refuted. Brand news moments do not move post views; as columns they hurt on YouTube and X.
