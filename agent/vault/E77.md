# E77 · How many earlier posts of an author are needed

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: how does the author-history gain grow with the number of earlier posts used (k = 1, 2, 3, 5, 10)? This is what Brand24 would need to store (Q8).
- Hypothesis: one earlier post gives most of the gain; more posts add a little.
- Refuted if: the gain keeps rising clearly with k.

## Setup
- Correction sum of the last k earlier non-repeat posts' over-performance / (k + a), a chosen on other brand folds (as E70). Two views: all posts with at least k earlier posts; the same posts (at least 5 earlier) with k ≤ 5 (fewer than 30 posts, not reported).

## Result
- k = 1: +0.104 (0.034-0.174) TikTok, 63 posts; +0.138 (0.089-0.195) YouTube, 379 posts; +0.065 (-0.007 to 0.124) X, 112 posts.
- YouTube k = 2: +0.170 (140 posts), k = 3: +0.163 (75 posts). X k = 2-3: about 0 (35-49 posts).
- Too few posts with 5+ earlier posts for a fixed-post curve.

## Decision
Partly supported. One earlier post gives a clear gain on TikTok and YouTube; on YouTube 2-3 posts may add a bit more, but the samples are small and the CIs overlap. On X the gain is uncertain. For Brand24: storing the last 1-3 views of an author is enough to test it.

## Follow-ups
- Repeat on a larger sample if Brand24 has author history (Q8).
