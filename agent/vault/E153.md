# E153 · Hand-check sample vs the full fetched sample

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the 130 hand-checked FB posts (E93) are a subset of the 402 fetched posts (E95). Is the hand sample
  representative, or is it easier?
- Hypothesis: the hand sample is close to the full one.
- Refuted if: the errors differ by a lot.

## Setup
- Both samples scored with the same transfer model (ridge on likes + comments, X + YouTube) and with the rule;
  overlap by `mention_id`; median views.
- Run: `python src/fb_sample_compare.py`.

## Result
| sample | n | model MAE | rule MAE | median views |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hand | 130 | 0.345 | 0.947 | 1 080 |
| full | 402 | 0.385 | 0.932 | 1 414 |

- Overlap: **130** (the hand sample is a subset of the full sample).

## Decision
The hand sample is slightly easier (0.345 vs 0.385) and smaller-viewed (median 1 080 vs 1 414), so E93's headline is a
little optimistic; the full-sample 0.385 is the number to report. The rule error is the same in both (0.93-0.95).
