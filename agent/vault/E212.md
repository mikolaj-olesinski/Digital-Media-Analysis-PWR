# E212 · Can we undo regression to the mean without losing MAE?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the transfer ridge under-predicts large posts. Part of that may be attenuation: likes are a noisy
  measurement of a post's audience, so the OLS likes slope is shrunk toward 0. Does an errors-in-variables correction
  or variance matching remove the tail bias at little MAE cost?
- Hypothesis (falsifiable): (a) instrumental-variable regression (export log likes / comments instrument the re-read
  log likes / comments, y = re-read views, on posts with both reads) gives a steeper likes slope; (b) variance matching
  p' = mean(p) + (p - mean(p)) · sd(y) / sd(p_oof), with the factor from brand-grouped 5-fold CV on the source, does the
  same. Either cuts the mean bias in the top quintile by predicted y by at least half, at an MAE cost of at most 0.01
  on FB / IG.
- Refuted if: the instrumented likes slope is within 5% of the OLS slope on the same posts, or MAE gets worse by more
  than 0.02 on FB or IG (primary variant).

## Setup
- Source: re-read X + YouTube (E210 loader: status ok, deduplicated, missing likes / comments dropped). Base = E210
  primary (ridge on C2, re-read X + YT): FB 0.387, IG 0.382.
- **Primary (fixed before running): (a) 2SLS on the X + YT posts with both reads; first stage regresses each re-read
  regressor on both export regressors; second stage y (re-read) on the fitted values; plain least squares. FB / IG
  predicted with the second-stage coefficients on their fresh reactions.**
- Variants, all reported: OLS on the same paired posts (direct comparator for the slope); reliability correction
  (likes slope / λ, λ = cov(export, re-read) / var(re-read), intercept refitted at the means); (b) variance matching
  centred on the target's own mean prediction (no target views used).
- Outputs: FB, IG, and leave-one-platform-out among re-read TikTok / YouTube / X (train on the other two, same
  methods fitted on the training platforms only) = 5 scores; MAE with CI, paired diff vs base (2,000 brand draws),
  top-quintile bias by predicted and by true y, brand-total median ratio and Kendall tau.
- Run: `.venv/bin/python src/deshrink.py` (seconds, local).

## Result

reliability λ = 0.78 (likes), 0.81 (comments), corrected likes slope 0.574; variance-matching factor 1.12.

## Decision
Refuted. The instrumented likes slope equals the OLS slope (0.97 on X + YT; 0.88-1.02 in the leave-one-out fits), so
noise in the like counts does not attenuate the slope; the under-prediction of large posts by true y is ordinary
regression to the mean of a conditional-mean model, not measurement error. The reliability correction treats the
export-vs-re-read difference (growth, λ 0.78) as noise and over-predicts large posts (FB +0.079, IG +0.040).
Variance matching trades MAE for totals: on FB / IG it brings brand totals near 1 (FB +0.07, IG -0.13 log10) and the
top-by-true bias down (FB 0.20 -> 0.02, IG 0.29 -> 0.14) at +0.007 / +0.011 MAE, but it hurts TikTok and YouTube
(+0.04 / +0.06) out of platform, so it is not a safe default. Keep the plain ridge as the point model; if totals are
the goal, variance matching is a documented option with this caveat.
