# E145 · FB tier calibration with pooled splits

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: E144 could not calibrate the >1M tier (fewer than 20 posts per fold). With 20 random 75/25 brand splits the
  calibration set is larger; does a per-tier quantile now fix the coverage?
- Hypothesis: yes, the large tier reaches about 90%.
- Refuted if: the large tier stays under-covered.

## Setup
- FB transfer CQR; 20 random brand splits (25% calibration, 75% test); per-tier conformity quantile, fallback to global
  when a tier has fewer than 20 calibration posts.
- Run: `python src/fb_cqr_pooled.py`.

## Result
| tier | n | global | per-tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| <10k | 50 | 0.905 | 0.903 |
| 10k-1M | 176 | 0.905 | 0.891 |
| **>1M** | 70 | **0.843** | **0.832** |

## Decision
Per-tier calibration still does not fix the large-page under-coverage; it makes it slightly worse (the tier quantile is
noisy). The gap is not a single width problem at this sample size. Practical fix: widen the >1M band by a fixed factor
(e.g. 1.3-1.5) and say so, or pool calibration across platforms. Do not claim a calibrated >1M band.
