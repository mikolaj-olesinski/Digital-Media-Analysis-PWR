# E157 · FB brand totals and the hybrid ranking

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: with 19 brands that have >= 5 FB posts, do the estimated brand totals hold in level and in order? Does the
  hybrid improve the ranking?
- Hypothesis: the order holds, the level is low (a sum of medians under-estimates a skewed total).
- Refuted if: the order is random or the hybrid improves it.

## Setup
- FB 402 posts; brand total = sum of 10^predicted (global or hybrid) vs the measured sum; brands with >= 5 posts.
- Ratio, share off by >2x, Kendall tau of the brand order.
- Run: `python src/fb_brand_totals.py`.

## Result
| model | median ratio | share off >2x | Kendall |
|---|---|---|---|
| global | -0.31 (x0.49) | 53% | **0.801** |
| hybrid | -0.30 | 53% | 0.789 |

## Decision
FB brand totals are **low by x2** (summing medians under-estimates a skewed total; the same as X, E109) and half the
brands are off by >2x, but the **order is good** (Kendall 0.80). The hybrid does not improve either. Apply the E109
one-number calibration to the FB totals before reporting them, and never report a total without an interval.
