# E191 · Third-party mention videos: the population closest to Brand24

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the earlier external samples were brand-centric (brand search plus the brand's own channel). Can we instead
  fetch **third-party** videos that mention a brand, including tiny and zero-view ones, and how does the model do on that
  population?
- Hypothesis: mention-like third-party videos are reachable, include zeros, and the model behaves closer to the export
  there than on brand-owned videos.
- Refuted if: third-party videos cannot be separated, or the model's error is as large as on brand channels.

## Setup
- Four mention-like queries per brand ("<brand> review", "unboxing", "haul", "honest review"), search 30 each.
- Remove videos from the brand's own channel: channel name contains the brand, or the channel dominates the results.
- Draw top 2, bottom 2 and 2 around the median by views; full metadata (views, likes, comments, channel followers, date),
  keep upload >= 2025-01-01 and duration <= 15 min.
- Model: CatBoost trained on the Brand24 YouTube rows (followers, likes, comments, flags, age).

## Result
| quantity | third-party mentions | Brand24 YouTube | previous external samples (E189/E190) |
|---|---|---|---|
| videos | **47** (12 brands) | 2,441 | 53 / 131 |
| zero-view | **1** | - | 0 |
| below 100 / below 1,000 views | **17 / 26** | - | 1 / - |
| median views | **362** | 433 | 6,697 / 11,489 |
| model MAE | 0.791 (bias +0.39) | 0.456 | 0.726 / 0.761 |
| MAE by stratum: low (median 5.5 views) | **0.690** (bias -0.29) | - | - |
| MAE by stratum: mid (median 2,638) | **0.441** (bias +0.42) | - | - |
| MAE by stratum: top (median 786,033) | **1.569** (bias +1.55) | - | 1.480 |
| median baseline MAE | 1.822 | - | 1.599 / 1.860 |

- The validation uses 45 rows (views > 0 and followers > 0); the zero-view video is reported in the sample but cannot enter
  a log-scale error.

## Decision
The mention-like population **is** reachable and includes zeros and 1-view videos; the previous samples were
brand-centric, so their x5 level shift was expected, not a model failure. On third-party mentions the model is close to
the export for small and mid posts (**0.441-0.690**) and only the popular stratum fails (1.569, ~x30 low). This corrects
E190: zero-view videos are not reachable through brand channels/search, but they are through obscure third-party videos.
The practical recipe for an external check: search mention-like queries, exclude the brand's own channel, and sample
across view strata.
