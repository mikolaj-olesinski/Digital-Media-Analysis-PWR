# E107 · Brand portfolio: what would change on X

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: on X, where Brand24's reach is a rule ×9 too high (E99), what would change for a client's brand totals if
  the reach were replaced by a views model? Brands with >= 5 posts, rule vs model vs measured views.
- Hypothesis: the model fixes the level but may not fix the order.
- Refuted if: the model is no better on brand totals.

## Setup
- X: 1 803 posts with views, 189 brands with >= 5 posts; TikTok 238 and YouTube 213 brands for comparison.
- Model: ridge on likes + comments, out-of-fold by brand; brand total = sum of predicted views (uncalibrated).
- Metrics per brand: ratio estimate/true, share off by >2x, median absolute log error, Kendall tau of the brand order,
  top-5 overlap.
- Run: `python src/portfolio_sim.py`.

## Result
| platform | median ratio b24 | median ratio model | off >2x b24 / model | median abs error b24 / model | Kendall b24 / model |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| X | **×11.2** | ×0.66 | **96% / 49%** | **1.05 / 0.30** | 0.63 / 0.58 |
| TikTok | ×1.0 (is views) | ×0.8 | 0% / 29% | 0.00 / 0.19 | 1.00 / 0.78 |
| YouTube | ×1.0 (is views) | ×0.6 | 0% / 50% | 0.00 / 0.29 | 1.00 / 0.65 |

- On X the model halves the share of brands off by >2x (96% -> 49%) and cuts the median brand error from 1.05 to 0.30,
  but its totals are low by ×1.5 (uncalibrated; E73 applies a one-number calibration) and the brand order is slightly
  worse (0.63 -> 0.58).
- On TikTok/YouTube the rule **is** the view count, so it cannot be improved for level or order.

## Decision
Replace the X level with a calibrated model; keep the existing ranking; leave TikTok/YouTube alone. For the product this
is the concrete "so what": the biggest gain is not a better model everywhere, it is fixing one platform's level.
