# E118 · Conformalized quantile intervals

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the raw 5-95% quantile intervals cover only 74% instead of 90% (E57). Does conformalized quantile regression
  (CQR) restore nominal coverage, and at what width?
- Hypothesis: CQR reaches about 90% with a modest widening.
- Refuted if: coverage stays far below 90% or the width explodes.

## Setup
- LightGBM quantile models (alpha 0.05 / 0.95) on the final features, 5-fold brand CV.
- Within each outer fold, a brand-grouped 75/25 split gives fit and calibration parts; conformity score
  max(q_lo - y, y - q_hi); the 90% quantile of the score widens the band.
- Baselines: raw quantile band; split conformal around the median model (E98 style).
- Run: `python src/cqr_intervals.py`.

## Result
| platform | raw coverage | CQR coverage | CQR width | split conformal coverage | split width |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.752 | **0.889** | 1.14 | 0.883 | 1.16 |
| YouTube | 0.742 | **0.902** | 1.71 | 0.893 | 1.92 |
| X | 0.739 | **0.891** | 2.07 | 0.894 | 2.03 |

## Decision
CQR restores 90% coverage on all three platforms and is narrower than split conformal on TikTok and YouTube. Use CQR
for the reported 90% interval; the raw quantile band should not be reported. The widths are wide (×14 / ×51 / ×117),
which is the honest cost of the error.
