# E92 · Brand effect carried over from other platforms

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: if a brand's posts with views are known on other platforms, does the brand's over-performance there carry over to the target platform?
- Hypothesis: big brands are big everywhere, so a shrunk brand effect helps.
- Refuted if: no gain.

## Setup
- Leave one platform out with brands kept in the sources (realistic when Brand24 has the brand on other platforms). Ridge on likes + comments; brand effect = mean residual of the brand's source posts × n / (n + k), k ∈ {1, 3, 10}, added to the target prediction.

## Result
- Gains: TikTok -0.065 / -0.048 / -0.023 (k = 1 / 3 / 10, CIs below zero); YouTube -0.008 to +0.001; X -0.022 to -0.007.

## Decision
Refuted. A brand's over-performance on one platform does not carry to another; it hurts. Consistent with E72 (brand explains at most 8% of the error) and E69 (cold start across brands).
