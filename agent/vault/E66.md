# E66 · Account type from the author name

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: are posts from the brand's own account, from a branch / dealer / fan account with the brand in its name, or from news / media accounts viewed differently, and does the label help the final model?
- Hypothesis: official accounts get fewer views per follower (followers are customers, not an audience); the label adds ≤ 0.005 next to reactions, like the AI type labels (E17).
- Refuted if: the label gains ≥ 0.01 with a CI above zero on any platform.

## Setup
- Built locally from the data, no model: official = author name or handle equals the brand name, optionally with a country / "official" / "jobs" suffix; brand in the name = brand name inside a longer name; news / media = media words in the name; other. Plus follower tiers.
- Per platform and label: median views, median views per follower, bias and MAE of the out-of-fold final model. One-hot label added to the final model, same brand folds, paired brand bootstrap.

## Result
- Shares: official 5.0% of Facebook posts, 1.2% YouTube, ≤ 0.4% elsewhere; brand in the name 2.6-3.8% on Facebook, TikTok, YouTube; news / media 7.0% of YouTube.
- Median views per follower, official vs other: TikTok 0.06 vs 0.60 (7 posts), YouTube 0.010 vs 0.020 (29), X 0.003 vs 0.065 (4).
- Yet the final model underestimates official accounts: bias -0.36 (TikTok), -0.52 (YouTube); MAE 0.39 / 0.77 vs 0.27 / 0.45 for other posts. Brand-in-name accounts are underestimated by 0.09 / 0.20 / 0.22.
- Follower tiers: views per follower fall from 3.7 (< 1k) to 0.02 (> 1M) on TikTok; similar on YouTube and X.
- As a feature: +0.001 / -0.000 / -0.001, all CIs include zero.

## Decision
Supported. Official and brand-named accounts are rare (≤ 5% of posts) and get few views per follower, but more views than their reactions suggest (people watch brand content without liking it), so the model underestimates them by ×2-3. Too few posts for the feature to move the overall MAE. Worth a sentence in the error analysis; not added to the model.

## Follow-ups
- With more brand-account posts (Facebook, Instagram after B14) the label may matter more.
