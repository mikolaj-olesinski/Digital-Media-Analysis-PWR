# E171 · Company facts from Wikidata

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: do company size and age (employees, revenue, founding year) explain views beyond reactions?
- Hypothesis: posts about bigger or older companies reach more people for the same reactions.
- Refuted if: paired gains with CIs at or below zero on TikTok / YouTube / X and no gain on the Facebook transfer.

## Setup
- `src/fetch_wikimedia_more.py wikidata`: Wikidata item of each of the 293 brand articles (293 found); employees (P1128),
  revenue in USD (P2139), inception (P571), latest value by point in time. Official social follower counts (P8687) were
  requested but none came back with a platform qualifier.
- Columns: log10 employees (31-34% of posts), log10 revenue, brand age in years at the post (80-83%); -1 when missing.
- Evaluation: final CatBoost (`em.ENG`) vs + columns, same brand folds, paired brand-bootstrap gain (TikTok / YouTube / X);
  Facebook: ridge on likes + comments (+ columns) trained on X + YouTube and scored on the 402 fetched FB posts, plus
  in-platform brand CV on those posts.
- Run: `.venv/bin/python src/fetch_wikimedia_more.py` (fetch), then `python src/external_brand.py` via Kueue.

## Result
| platform | final model | + facts | gain (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.276 | -0.002 (-0.005 to 0.000) |
| YouTube | 0.456 | 0.462 | **-0.007** (-0.011 to -0.002) |
| X | 0.467 | 0.469 | -0.003 (-0.007 to 0.002) |

- Largest residual correlation: revenue on X, Spearman 0.13 (on the third of posts with revenue); others |ρ| < 0.06.
- Attention + facts together: -0.003 / -0.014 / -0.009.
- Facebook transfer MAE 0.385 → 0.388 (in-platform CV 0.384 → 0.383).

## Decision
Refuted. Company size and age add nothing beyond reactions and followers; YouTube gets worse.
