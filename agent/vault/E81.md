# E81 · How Facebook and Instagram posts look next to the platforms with views

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: which platform with views do FB / IG posts resemble in reaction structure, and how far are they from all of them?
- Hypothesis: Instagram resembles TikTok (short video, many likes); Facebook resembles YouTube.
- Refuted if: the adversarial AUC is lowest for another source.

## Setup
- All 2,500 posts per platform, and FB / IG videos and Reels only. Medians of likes, comments and shares per like, likes per 1k followers, followers coverage, language, text length. Adversarial CatBoost (5-fold stratified) telling a target (FB / IG videos and Reels) from a source (all posts) on log likes, log comments, comments per like, plus text length or followers. AUC 0.5 = indistinguishable.

## Result
- FB videos: median 6 likes, 24% without reactions, followers known for 75%, 0.06 likes per 1k followers (TikTok 21, YouTube 0.24). IG Reels: median 1 like, 44% without reactions, followers known for 9%, no shares column.
- AUC on likes + comments: IG vs X 0.65, vs YouTube 0.66, vs TikTok 0.85; FB vs X 0.81, vs YouTube 0.82, vs TikTok 0.87.
- With followers FB is almost fully separable: 0.89 (YouTube) to 0.98 (TikTok).

## Decision
Refuted. In reactions Instagram looks most like X and YouTube, not TikTok; Facebook is far from every source, especially once followers are used (pages with huge followings and very few likes). Transfer to FB should use likes and comments only, not followers.
