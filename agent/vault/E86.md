# E86 · Brand popularity on Wikipedia

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: does an external measure of brand popularity (monthly English Wikipedia pageviews of the brand's article) explain views beyond reactions, as a brand-level signal the Brand24 file lacks?
- Hypothesis: posts about popular brands over-perform, and pageviews in the post's month help as columns.
- Refuted if: gains with CIs at or below zero.

## Setup
- `src/fetch_wikipedia.py` (user consent on 2026-11-05; only brand names were sent): Wikipedia search for '<brand> company', top article with a name check, then Wikimedia monthly pageviews 2024-01 to 2026-10. 320 of 348 brands passed the name check; 27 more were removed after a hand review of the titles (wrong article, e.g. a disambiguation page or a building), leaving 293 brands and 84% of posts.
- Columns: log10 pageviews in the post's month, the brand's median, and their difference (-1 when no article). Final model + these columns, same brand folds; Spearman of the brand median with brand mean over-performance and mean log views (brands with ≥ 5 posts).

## Result
- Spearman with brand mean log views: 0.18 / 0.12 / 0.05; with brand over-performance: 0.15 / -0.07 / 0.08 (157-200 brands).
- As columns: 0.000 (-0.003 to 0.003) / -0.010 (-0.014 to -0.005) / -0.010 (-0.017 to -0.004).

## Decision
Refuted. Brand popularity outside social media barely relates to the views of posts about the brand and makes the model worse on YouTube and X. Consistent with E72: brands explain at most 8% of the error, authors most of it.
