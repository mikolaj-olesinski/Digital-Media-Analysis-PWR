# E112 · How many posts does a brand total need?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: reach is dominated by a few posts (E96). How unstable is a brand total computed from n posts?
- Hypothesis: with 20 posts the total is roughly stable; with 5 it is not.
- Refuted if: even 5 posts give a stable total.

## Setup
- Brands with >= 20 measured posts (39 brands, 41 brand-platform groups; TikTok, YouTube, X).
- For n = 5, 10, 20: 400 bootstrap draws of n posts, total views; p90/p10 spread of the total and share of draws within
  2x of the full-brand total.
- Run: `python src/brand_stability.py`.

## Result
| posts sampled | median p90/p10 | median share within 2x of the brand total |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **×53** | 17% |
| 10 | ×21 | 37% |
| 20 | ×6.4 | 66% |

## Decision
A brand total from a handful of posts is not a measurement. Even 20 posts leave a ×6 spread and only two thirds of the
totals within 2x. For Brand24 this argues for intervals (E109), for reporting the number of posts behind a total, and
against comparing brands on small samples.

## Follow-ups
- A sequential rule: stop adding posts when the bootstrap spread falls below a threshold.
- Does the stability change if the top post is excluded (tail-trimmed total)?
