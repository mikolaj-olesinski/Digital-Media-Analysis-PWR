# E160 · FB brand interval with at least 10 posts

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: the 80% FB brand interval covers 0.737 with 19 brands (E158/E159). Does restricting to brands with >= 10
  posts (14 brands) fix the coverage?
- Hypothesis: more posts per brand give a more stable interval.
- Refuted if: coverage does not improve.

## Setup
- Same leave-one-brand-out calibration and width, restricted to brands with >= 10 posts; constant and n-scaled widths.
- Run: `python src/fb_brand_interval.py`.

## Result
| subset | brands | coverage const | coverage scaled |
|---|---|---|---|
| >= 5 posts | 19 | 0.737 | 0.789 |
| **>= 10 posts** | 14 | **0.714** | **0.714** |

## Decision
Restricting to brands with more posts does not help (coverage 0.714); the problem is the number of brands, not the
number of posts. Do not claim calibrated FB brand intervals.
