# E202 · Instagram transfer on 500 hand-collected reels

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Does the X + YouTube model, never trained on Instagram, estimate Instagram reel views from likes and comments read at the same moment, and how close is it to a model trained on Instagram itself?
- Hypothesis: It works like Facebook (E93, x2.4) with a level shift; calibration on 25 posts removes the shift.

## Setup
- 500 random reels from the Brand24 IG slice (11 brands; the slice has only 11). Read by a browser agent on a team member's logged-in account (read-only, values from the web API). 368 with views, 132 gone, 30 with hidden likes.
- Ridge on log likes + log comments trained on X + YouTube (as E93). Comments-only ridge for hidden likes. 25-post median shift (200 draws). Own-platform reference: ridge on IG with brand-grouped 5-fold CV. `python src/ig_transfer.py` (seconds).

## Result
| | posts | MAE [95% CI] | typical error | within x2 | under / over |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| X + YouTube -> IG, likes + comments | 338 | **0.424** [0.379, 0.494] | **x2.65** | 41% | 238 / 100 |
| + 25-post shift | 313 | 0.382 | x2.41 | 48% | |
| IG trained on IG (brand CV) | 338 | 0.372 [0.327, 0.422] | x2.36 | 49% | 172 / 166 |
| without the 3 largest brands | 189 | 0.469 [0.412, 0.534] | x2.95 | 35% | 130 / 59 |
| comments only (hidden likes) | 30 | 1.048 | x11.2 | 10% | 27 / 3 |
| comments only (same 338 posts) | 338 | 0.791 | x6.2 | 20% | 277 / 61 |

- Median bias +0.25: reels get ~x1.8 more views per reaction than X + YouTube posts; 98 posts under by more than x3, 24 over.
- Spearman 0.88 (ranking is good; the level is off).
- Top 3 brands hold 44% of posts. Per-brand MAE 0.32-0.59 (10 brands with >= 10 posts); 8 of 10 under-predicted.

## Decision
Supported. Instagram behaves like Facebook: zero-shot x2.65, within 0.05 of a model trained on Instagram itself once 25 posts fix the level (x2.41 vs x2.36). Likes are required: without them the error is x6-11.
