# E129 · Distributional model vs CQR

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: a distributional model (CatBoost RMSEWithUncertainty) gives a mean and a variance per post. Is its 90% band
  better than the MAE point model with CQR?
- Hypothesis: the Gaussian band is narrower and about as accurate.
- Refuted if: the band under-covers or the mean is worse.

## Setup
- CatBoost `RMSEWithUncertainty` (300 iterations) on the final features, 5-fold brand CV.
- 90% band = mean +- 1.645 sd; MAE of the mean; comparison with E118 (CQR).
- Run: `python src/dist_uncertainty.py`.

## Result
| platform | MAE mean | coverage 90% | width | CQR coverage / width |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.276 | 0.871 | 1.01 | 0.889 / 1.14 |
| YouTube | 0.459 | 0.884 | 1.67 | 0.902 / 1.71 |
| X | 0.471 | 0.864 | 1.87 | 0.891 / 2.07 |

## Decision
The distributional band is narrower but under-covers (0.86-0.88 at nominal 90%) and the mean is slightly worse on MAE
(0.276/0.459/0.471 vs 0.273/0.456/0.467). CQR remains the interval method. No gain.
