# E156 · FB error by industry

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: is the FB residual concentrated in an industry?
- Hypothesis: sportswear/media are the hardest, as on TikTok/X (E152).
- Refuted if: no industry spread.

## Setup
- Hybrid FB predictions (global <1M, large-page model >1M); absolute error on log10 views by industry (>= 20 posts).
- Run: `python src/fb_brand_totals.py`.

## Result
- Best: beverages 0.263, finance and insurance 0.334, fashion 0.374.
- Worst: **sportswear 0.597**, tech and software 0.408.

## Decision
The FB spread is 0.26-0.60, with sportswear the hardest — consistent with E152 (viral-prone categories). Samples per
industry are small (>= 20 posts), so treat this as a segment caveat, not a feature.
