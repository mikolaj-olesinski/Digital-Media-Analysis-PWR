# E228 · Other views of the same-platform result: without extreme posts, as classification, as probability

## Question and hypothesis
- Question (slides): how large is the error without the smallest / largest posts, and does the result work as a three-range classification or as a probability of a large post?
- Hypothesis: cutting by the estimate (usable) gains much less than cutting by true views (oracle), because the model compresses extremes (E51, E57, E180).

## Setup
- Final CatBoost predictions, same platform (`results_preds.csv`, E55 component folds): TT 2491, YT 2441, X 1803.
- Error ×10^MAE without the bottom / top 20% and on the middle 60%, cut by true views and by the estimate.
- Three ranges (<1k, 1k-10k, >10k) from the regression: accuracy, balanced accuracy, majority baseline.
- ROC AUC of the estimate for top 20% and bottom 20% of true views.
- `src/size_slices.py` (seconds, local).

## Results
- All posts: ×1.87 / ×2.90 / ×3.03.
- Cut by true views (oracle): middle 60% ×1.69 / ×2.43 / ×2.29; without smallest ×1.82 / ×2.70 / ×2.75; without largest ×1.78 / ×2.73 / ×2.71.
- Cut by the estimate (usable): middle 60% ×1.77 / ×2.95 / ×2.93; without smallest ×1.81 / ×2.75 / ×2.99; without largest ×1.85 / ×3.10 / ×3.00.
- Three ranges: accuracy 0.82 / 0.80 / 0.95, majority 0.59 / 0.63 / 0.95, balanced accuracy 0.77 / 0.72 / 0.43.
- AUC large post 0.97 / 0.92 / 0.84; small post 0.93 / 0.92 / 0.79.

## Decision
Supported. The extremes carry the error, but they cannot be removed in use: cutting by the estimate gains <= 0.1 factor. Ranges work on TT / YT; on X the 95% accuracy equals the majority guess. As a ranking (large vs rest) the estimate is strong (AUC 0.84-0.97). Presentation only, no change to the model.
