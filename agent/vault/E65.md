# E65 · GraphSAGE on the text-similarity graph

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: hand-made graph features gave ≤ 0.009 (E03, E07, E29). Does a graph neural network that learns how to aggregate neighbours (GraphSAGE, mean aggregator) do better than the same network without the graph?
- Hypothesis (low prior): GraphSAGE beats the MLP by ≤ 0.01 and does not beat CatBoost; the mean of CatBoost and GraphSAGE gains ≤ 0.01.
- Refuted if: GraphSAGE or the mean beats CatBoost by ≥ 0.02 on any platform.

## Setup
- Graph per platform: 10 nearest neighbours by cosine on the e5 embeddings (E50), symmetrised, row-normalised; all posts of the platform (transductive on text, no labels).
- Node features: final-model inputs (ENG) + embedding PCA 16, standardised on the training fold.
- Two-layer GraphSAGE (64 hidden, dropout 0.2, AdamW 3e-3, 400 full-batch epochs, L1 loss on training-fold nodes only), 3 seeds averaged; the same network without neighbour terms (MLP); final CatBoost; means of CatBoost with each network (the MLP mean is the control for an ensemble effect). 5-fold brand CV, paired brand bootstrap CI.

## Result
| | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| CatBoost (final model) | 0.273 | 0.456 | 0.466 |
| MLP (same features, no graph) | 0.274 | 0.456 | 0.467 |
| GraphSAGE (text graph) | 0.279 | 0.467 | 0.474 |
| GraphSAGE vs MLP | -0.006 (-0.011 to -0.001) | -0.011 (-0.020 to -0.002) | -0.006 (-0.015-0.003) |
| mean CatBoost + MLP (control) vs CatBoost | **+0.008** (0.005-0.011) | **+0.016** (0.009-0.021) | **+0.016** (0.008-0.022) |
| mean CatBoost + GraphSAGE vs CatBoost | +0.007 | +0.015 | +0.016 |
| GraphSAGE mean vs MLP mean | -0.001 | -0.000 | +0.000 |

## Decision
Hypothesis supported for the graph: GraphSAGE is worse than the same network without neighbours (-0.006 to -0.011), confirming the earlier graph results (E03, E07, E29): text neighbours carry no view signal beyond the post's own inputs. The surprise is the control: a plain MLP on the final-model inputs + 16 embedding components, averaged with CatBoost, gains +0.008 / +0.016 / +0.016, more than TabPFN (E47). The MLP alone equals CatBoost but errs differently, so the average helps. This is the strongest simple ensemble found so far.
