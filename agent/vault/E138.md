# E138 · Robustness to missing reactions

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: client data is often incomplete. If likes/comments are missing at prediction time, how much does the MAE
  degrade?
- Hypothesis: a moderate degradation, worse on TikTok where reactions matter most.
- Refuted if: the model is insensitive.

## Setup
- Train on clean data (5-fold brand CV); at prediction, set likes and comments to missing (with the missing flag) for a
  random 10/25/50/100% of test posts; 5 folds.
- Run: `python src/missing_robust.py`.

## Result
| platform | 0% | 10% | 25% | 50% | 100% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 0.273 | 0.350 | 0.459 | 0.641 | 1.005 |
| YouTube | 0.456 | 0.505 | 0.570 | 0.686 | 0.929 |
| X | 0.467 | 0.484 | 0.503 | 0.542 | 0.607 |

## Decision
Completeness matters: 10% missing already costs 0.03-0.08 MAE, 50% costs 0.08-0.37, and a fully missing test is much
worse than a model trained without reactions (E130: 0.60 vs 1.00 on TikTok). X is the most robust, TikTok the most
fragile. For incomplete data, widen the band or use a model trained with the missing pattern; do not present the clean
MAE.
