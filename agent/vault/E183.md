# E183 · Creator score: is an author's over-performance stable over time?

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: Can an author's views beyond the reaction-based estimate be used as a creator score (influencer selection)?
- Hypothesis: The author's mean residual in the first half of their posts predicts the second half.

## Setup
- Authors with >= 4 distinct posts on a platform; mean out-of-fold residual of the first vs the second half of their posts (by time).
- Part of the critical review batch: `python src/critical_review.py` (via Kueue; E178 locally).

## Result
- YouTube: 23 authors (127 posts): Spearman 0.81, 91% on the same side of the median, top-20% list precision 0.60, first-half top 20% still +0.78 log10 in the second half.
- TikTok 2 and X 7 authors with >= 4 posts: not measurable.

## Decision
Supported on YouTube only, on a tiny sample. The author effect is stable enough for a creator score, but the Brand24 sample has too few repeat authors; it needs Brand24's archive (question 8).
