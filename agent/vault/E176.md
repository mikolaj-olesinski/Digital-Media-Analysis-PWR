# E176 · One estimator for whatever fields arrive, with calibrated confidence

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: if a post arrives without reactions, without followers or without both, can a router pick a model trained
  for that input pattern and still give calibrated 50% / 90% bands and a calibrated probability of being within x2?
- Hypothesis: per-pattern CQR stays at nominal coverage; the full model fed missing reactions does not.
- Refuted if: per-pattern 90% coverage falls below 0.87 or the probability within x2 is off by more than 0.05 on average.

## Setup
- Patterns: full (ENG, + age on X); no reactions (followers + text length, language, labels, month, age); no followers
  (reactions + flags, + age on X); nothing (text length, language, labels, month, age).
- Per pattern: CatBoost MAE point, LightGBM quantiles 0.05/0.25/0.75/0.95, CQR on 25% of the training brands. Probability
  within x2 = share of calibration posts with |error| < log10 2 in the same quintile of raw band width.
- Brand 5-fold CV. Baseline without a router: the full model fed the post with reactions set missing. Mixed test: 60%
  full, 20% no reactions, 10% no followers, 10% nothing, routed by available fields.
- Run: `python src/fallback_estimator.py` via Kueue.

- Every pattern keeps 90% coverage at 0.884-0.900 and 50% at 0.46-0.51; the bands widen honestly as fields disappear.
- Without a router, missing reactions break the bands: coverage 0.46 / 0.56 / 0.83 and the x2 probability claims 0.60
  while 0.12 is observed (TikTok).
- The probability within x2 is calibrated on average per pattern (|predicted - observed| <= 0.02), but within a pattern
  it does not rank posts: Brier equals the constant rate (0.224 vs 0.223 TikTok full). Its information is the pattern
  itself (mix: 0.227 vs 0.245).

## Decision
Adopted as the product shape: route by available fields, report point + 50% / 90% band + the pattern's probability of
being within x2. Never feed missing reactions to the full model. The per-post probability adds nothing beyond the pattern
and the band width; report it per pattern.
