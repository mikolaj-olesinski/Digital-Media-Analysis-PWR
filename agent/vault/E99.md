# E99 · Where Brand24's reach rule fails

## Question and hypothesis
- Question: on which posts is Brand24's `b24_estimated_reach` far from the measured views? The rule is
  `max(k × reactions, c(followers) × followers)`.
- Hypothesis: it is exact where the platform exports views (TikTok, YouTube) and wrong mainly on X, with a pattern by
  account and reaction size.
- Refuted if: the error is uniform across segments.

## Setup
- 6 571 posts with views > 0 and a positive reach (TikTok 2 491, YouTube 2 441, X 1 639).
- Error = log10(reach / views); a positive median means the rule over-estimates.
- Segments: platform; on X only, follower tier, reaction tier, age of the check, language. Segments with < 30 posts dropped.
- Run: `python src/b24_segments.py`.

## Result
| platform | posts | median log10(reach/views) | MAE | share off by >2x |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 2 491 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0% |
| YouTube | 2 441 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0% |
| **X** | 1 639 | **+0.97 (x9.3)** | **1.08** | **87%** |

On X, by segment:
- followers: <1k x4.5 (MAE 0.99), 1k-10k **x16.6** (1.20), 10k-100k x14.8 (1.13), 100k-1M x16.2 (1.37).
- reactions: 0 -> x2.3 (0.80), **1-10 -> x27** (1.41), 11-100 -> x12.6 (1.03).
- age of the check: no trend (x7-11 in every bucket).
- language: English and other behave the same.

## Decision
The rule is a copy of the view count on TikTok and YouTube, and a x9 over-estimate on X, worst for posts with 1-10
reactions (x27) and mid-sized accounts (x16). For Brand24 this is a concrete confidence flag: X reach should be
flagged or replaced by a model; the error is not spread evenly, so a segment-level correction is possible.
