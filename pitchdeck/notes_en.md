# Pitch deck: notes

## What was done
- 7 slides, 16:9, generated by the `build.py` script (python-pptx): `pitchdeck_en.pptx` + `pitchdeck_en.pdf` (Polish version: `pitchdeck_pl.*`; English texts live in `strings_en.py`).
- Layout follows the instructor's template (email of 7 Oct, the "5 slide rule" in its research version):
  1 title + hook · 2 context: platforms hide reach · 3 **hypothesis** (+ when it is refuted) ·
  4 **technology concept** (4 steps + data strip) · 5 **client = society** (5 groups) ·
  6 **impact** (a playful estimate: the funnel) · 7 **moat = feasibility + expertise** (data + state of knowledge).
  The former slides "What we lose", "What will come of it" (its visual moved to the hypothesis slide) and "Semester plan" were removed.
- Palette: deep purple, coral, yellow, blue, mint on a cream background; Calibri; titles 38 pt, text 20–28 pt.
  Icons and diagrams are built from shapes, no clip art.
- Effects: **Morph** transitions (the coloured circles from the title slide drift into the corners of the following slides, the progress bar at the bottom grows), **entrance animations** (cards fly in from below one after another, automatically, no clicking), soft shadows, gradients on coloured elements, a gradient background on the title slide.
  Morph needs PowerPoint 2019/365; older versions show a plain fade. The PDF shows the final state of the slides.
- Speaker notes on every slide with an estimated time.
- The Polish slides were rendered to PNG by PowerPoint (`render/pl/`) and inspected after every change. The English PDF was exported from PowerPoint; English PNG renders were not made.

## Numbers on the slides (from the script, from `data/raw/reach_samples_students.csv`)
- 12,500 mentions, 2,500 from each of 5 platforms (slide 5). Share of mentions with known views (`view_count`): TikTok 100%, YouTube 99%, X/Twitter 75%, Facebook 0%, Instagram 0% (computed in `build.py`).
- The value of the Brand24 constant and any model results are not given (there are none yet).
- The "today vs our idea" visual on slide 3 illustrates an idea, it is not data.
- Impact (slide 6): 5.24 bn accounts, DataReportal/Kepios, Digital 2025 (identities, not unique people); 2,400+ companies, Brand24's own claim (brand24.com/customers; external trackers report fewer); ~12,000 people, an assumption of 5 report readers per company (a playful estimate, not graded by the instructor).
- State of knowledge (slide 7): Cha et al. 2010 (ICWSM, "million follower fallacy"); Szabo and Huberman 2010 (Communications of the ACM, predicting content popularity); Bakshy et al. 2011 (WSDM, influence on Twitter, single cascades hard to predict); Angelopoulos and Bates 2023 (Foundations and Trends in ML, introduction to conformal prediction). Citations come from the agent's knowledge: check the full bibliographic entries before the preprint.

## Not verified
- "Platforms hide reach because they sell ads" and "Brand24 has to estimate with followers × constant" come from the task context and were not checked against sources.
- The consequences on slide 3 (blind assessment, wrong comparisons, late reaction, reports) are reasoning, not research.
- The semester plan (slide 7) came from the schedule in `docs/pl/course_rules.pdf`.
- Appearance outside PowerPoint (Google Slides, LibreOffice) was not checked.
